## The field we play in

Purpose-driven offers. Membership organisations, conscious products, regenerative and world-positive technology, and the transformations they sell.

The people who buy in this field share a shape: open to new ideas, suspicious of hype, moved by meaning and proof rather than by discount and urgency. Most direct-response tactics still work here, but the tone that carries them is different. Pressure reads as manipulation. Specificity and evidence read as respect.

This file makes the general field concrete. Rewrite it per client. The knowledge base stays the same.
