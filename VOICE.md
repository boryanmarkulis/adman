# Voice

Empty on purpose. The knowledge base carries the creative method; this file carries the sound.
Fill it from real writing that already worked, never from an AI-generated sample.

## How to fill it (30 minutes, once)

1. Collect 15 to 30 pieces of real copy that already went out and performed: posts, captions, emails, replies, landing-page sections. Real, shipped, yours.
2. Sort them by channel. A caption and a sales email are different instruments.
3. For each channel, note in plain words: sentence length, how it opens, how it closes, what it never does.
4. Paste 3 short verbatim examples per channel below. Verbatim, not paraphrased. Paraphrasing is how voice dies.

## Standing rules (apply before anything below)

- No emojis. No em dashes. No corporate jargon.
- No filler openers: "Certainly", "Great question", "In today's world", "Let's dive in".
- No hedging stacks: "it's important to note that", "arguably", "in many ways".
- Read it aloud. If you would not say it out loud to one person, rewrite it.
- Run `skills/copywriting/references/read-aloud-pass.md` on anything that ships.

## AI tells to hunt and kill

Check every draft for these before it leaves:

- Tricolon everywhere ("faster, cleaner, smarter").
- "It's not X, it's Y" as a crutch.
- Rhetorical question openers.
- Symmetrical paragraph lengths.
- Vague intensifiers: truly, deeply, incredibly, seamlessly, robust.
- A closing line that summarises instead of landing.
