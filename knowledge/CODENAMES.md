# Codenames

`case-*` pages come from real campaigns with the brands removed. The numbers, tests and conclusions are real; only the identities are gone.

| Codename | What it was |
|---|---|
| the beauty brand | Direct-to-consumer beauty brand. The deepest UGC, short-form and creative-testing material here. |
| the hero product | That brand's flagship product. |
| the footcare brand | Direct-to-consumer health product. Positioning and identity copy. |
| the wellness gadget | Physical wellness product. Product-test heuristics. |
| the solar installer | Service business in an emerging market. Local content and trust building. |
| the US solar company | Residential energy lead generation. Paid social lead ads at volume. |
| the lead agency | Lead-gen agency. Campaign structure and volume testing. |
| the studio | The operator's own studio. |
| the operator | The person whose five years of testing produced this. |

Ecommerce product names that survived (Fidgi Pen, Chameleon Shorts, Bug Snatcher and similar) are public dropship products, not clients. They stay because the product-selection reasoning around them is the lesson.
