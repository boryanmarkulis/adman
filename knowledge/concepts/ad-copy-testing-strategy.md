# Ad Copy Testing Strategy

Testing ad copy isn't about guessing what works. It's about systematically validating claims that resonate with real homeowner frustrations, especially those tied to checkable, personal experiences.

## The "Used Less, Paid More" Hook

The most powerful hook in our testing arsenal is the "used less, paid more" claim. It taps directly into a verified pain point: homeowners who reduced energy usage but saw their bills go up. This isn't hypothetical. It's a recurring theme in the Reddit corpus, where users consistently report confusion and anger over rising bills despite lower consumption.

This hook works because it's instantly verifiable. A reader can check their own bill in under ten seconds to confirm whether they're experiencing the same anomaly. That immediacy builds trust faster than any generic claim about savings or efficiency.

## Why This Hook Wins in Testing

When tested against other variations, this hook consistently outperforms generic value propositions like "save money" or "go solar." It doesn't just promise change; it reflects a real-world contradiction that many customers already feel. The emotional weight of being unfairly charged, combined with the ability to confirm it themselves, makes this copy highly actionable.

This pattern reinforces a broader truth: the best ad copy isn't invented. It's extracted from real customer language and validated through data. Testing should prioritize claims that are both emotionally resonant and factually checkable.

## Linking to Related Strategies

It also aligns with geo specificity in copy when tailored to regions with known rate hikes or utility fee structures.
