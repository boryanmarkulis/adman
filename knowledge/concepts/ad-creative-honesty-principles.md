# Ad Creative Honesty Principles

Honest ad creatives in clean energy avoid making specific guarantees and instead focus on empowering customers with tools for informed decision-making.

## Avoiding Specific Outcome Claims

Ads don't promise exact savings or fixed system prices. Instead, they position solar as a "yardstick" for comparison, helping users understand what's possible based on their unique situation. This approach keeps messaging truthful while still driving engagement by focusing on personal relevance over false promises.

## Using Fictional News Framing Ethically

Creatives use fictional mastheads like "NEW ENGLAND ENERGY REPORT / the lead agency" to mimic the look of news content, but deliberately avoid any real brand identifiers. No logos, bylines, or journalist names appear. This preserves the aesthetic of credibility without deceiving viewers about source authenticity.

## Staying Aligned with Clean Energy Values

This method supports broader principles in sustainable marketing: transparency, long-term trust, and real user empowerment. It aligns with [[ad-creative-strategy]], [[ad-content-strategy]], and [[ad-creative-structure]] by building campaigns that don't rely on exaggeration or misleading claims.

## Maintaining Integrity in Testing Loops

Even during rapid ad testing, the team never introduces false data points or fake testimonials. The creative testing loop remains grounded in real-world context, ensuring every variation respects user intelligence and avoids manipulative framing. This supports [[ad-creative-testing-strategy]] and [[ad-creative-optimization]].
