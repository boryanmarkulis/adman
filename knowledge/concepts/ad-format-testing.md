# Ad Format Testing

Testing new ad formats is a core driver of performance gains in clean energy marketing. The best formats combine authentic customer content with simple, high-impact hooks.

## What Works: UGC + Simple Hooks

The most effective ad format we've tested uses real customer testimonials paired with minimal copy and strong visual b-roll. This combo has delivered double the click-through rate and cut cost per click by over half compared to traditional formats. ROAS has also been significantly higher, proving that authenticity beats polish.

## Why It Works: B-Roll Carries the Message

It's not just the words. The visuals matter just as much. Specific b-roll clips from real customers have become brand carriers on their own. Footage of two real customers using the product has driven scale and recognition far beyond what copy alone could achieve. These moments feel genuine and resonate deeper than scripted content.

## Performance at Scale: Watch the Curve

Even when a format performs well initially, its long-term viability at scale needs monitoring. One longform UGC ad that showed strong early results is still holding up with a high click-through rate, but we're cautious about assuming it will maintain momentum. That's why testing isn't a one-time event. It's an ongoing loop.

## Testing Strategy: Start Simple, Iterate Fast

The key is to test variations quickly and drop what doesn't scale. Focus on formats that use real customer content, keep the copy lean, and let strong visuals do the work. Use the same b-roll across multiple ad variations to track performance consistency. Always tie results back to core metrics like CTR, CPC, and ROAS.
