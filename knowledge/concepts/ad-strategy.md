# Ad Strategy

A repeatable ad strategy using a $25 ABO, 3 ads, and a new pixel with behavior-based targeting is the most effective way to validate product-market fit quickly while isolating real funnel bottlenecks.

## Core Framework

The foundation of this strategy is a standardized testing setup that removes noise and enables clear decision-making:
- **$25 ABO (average budget per objective)**: This level balances data quality with speed. It's enough to gather meaningful signals without overcommitting early.
- **3 ads per ad set**: Multiple creatives test different hooks, visuals, and angles while keeping the environment controlled. This allows for variation within a consistent structure.
- **New pixel**: A clean tracking setup avoids contamination from prior campaigns or lookalike audiences built on outdated behavior.

This framework was used across three product tests: VelvaWax™, the beauty brand™ brush, and Birkmallows™ pillow slides, proving its repeatability.

## Targeting Consistency

All tests rely on **interest or video behavior targeting**, which captures users actively engaging with relevant content. This is far more effective than broad demographic targeting for early validation.
- the beauty brand™ test used one interest/video behavior per ad set, keeping the audience narrow and focused.
- Birkmallows™ expanded to include CA/US audiences, showing the model scales regionally without losing structure.

## Messaging Focus

The strategy isn't about message repetition. It's about using a reliable testing engine to validate different value propositions:
- **VelvaWax™**: Focused on ease, affordability, and painless removal.
- **the beauty brand™ brush**: Highlighted healthy hair and a lower price point ($19.95).
- **Birkmallows™**: Emphasized comfort and style of pillow slides.

The core mechanics stay fixed; the messaging adapts to product-specific benefits.

## Beyond Lead Volume: The Real Bottleneck

Evidence from later campaigns shows that top-of-funnel reach isn't the issue. The real constraint is conversion from quote to close.
- Pausing the solar installer Facebook organic group posting was based on data showing reach works, but the funnel breaks between quote and close.
- The true value of the ad campaign isn't in cheap leads, but in exposing that low-intent leads signal a deeper problem: lack of qualifying questions and speed-to-lead automation.

This insight shifts focus from buying more traffic to building a smarter system. The difference isn't volume; it's quality. One lead who already sent their electric bill is worth nine cold calls.

## Why This Works

By standardizing variables (budget, pixel, ad count), performance differences can be tied directly to creative or messaging. This reduces noise and enables rapid iteration.
The framework isn't just for ads. It's a **product-market fit testing engine** that can scale into broader campaigns once validated.
