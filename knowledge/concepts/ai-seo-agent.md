# AI SEO Agent

An AI SEO agent automates the creation and optimization of web content to boost search visibility, drive traffic, and strengthen internal link authority across a site.

## Core Capabilities

AI SEO agents generate high-volume blog and landing content at scale, targeting specific keywords and topics. They don't just write text; they structure it to reinforce key pages through strategic internal linking. For example, HarborSEO.ai has published 2,000 pages in one case study, generating 740k impressions and 13k clicks within a single video demonstration.

## Full-Stack Automation

Advanced agents like Edward handle end-to-end SEO workflows for local and niche websites. This includes researching target markets, writing localized content, building internal links, adapting copy for regional variations, publishing to platforms like Wix, and tracking performance all without human intervention. Edward is specifically designed as a Wix-native tool, supporting full autopilot from research to publication.

## Strategic Impact

These agents act as force multipliers in digital marketing by systematically increasing a site's authority and visibility. When used with clean energy or solar-focused content, they can drive qualified leads to landing pages while maintaining SEO integrity across thousands of pages.
