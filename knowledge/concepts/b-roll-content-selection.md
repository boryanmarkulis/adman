# B-roll content selection

Great B-roll grabs attention and keeps it, but only if it feels fresh and unexpected.

## Avoid repetition to maintain engagement

Reusing the same B-roll clips too often kills momentum. When content starts feeling familiar or stale, viewers disengage. The pattern of repetition can make even strong visuals feel dull over time. It's not just about variety for its own sake; it's about keeping the audience emotionally hooked.

## Find new content through active exploration

The best sources for fresh B-roll are platforms like TikTok and YouTube. Scrolling through TikTok is especially effective because it surfaces raw, real-time moments that feel authentic and urgent. Tools like sort TikTok downloader help capture these clips quickly so they don't get lost. The key is to be proactive, not passive.

## Organize by concept for faster reuse

Instead of hoarding random videos, group B-roll by theme. Categories like *salon extensions*, *hair damage*, *founder stories*, or *before-and-after transformations* make it easy to pull the right clip when you need it. This system turns your content library into a living toolkit, not a cluttered archive.

## Prioritize shock and novelty

The most effective B-roll isn't just relevant. It's surprising. Clips that show something extreme or unexpected stand out: hair pulled from drains, people getting their hair cut off in public, or messy salon fails. These moments trigger curiosity and emotion, which boosts retention and shares. Don't aim for polished perfection. Aim for visceral impact.
