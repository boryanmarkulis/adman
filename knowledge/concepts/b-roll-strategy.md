# B-roll strategy

Great B-roll isn't just filler; it's a storytelling engine that drives engagement and virality.

## The power of novelty in B-roll

The most compelling B-roll comes from fresh, unexpected visuals that surprise the viewer. It's not about stock footage or recycled clips. Instead, it's about discovering new moments that feel authentic and revealing. For example, capturing raw, behind-the-scenes details like a founder's early struggles or a real-time problem-solving moment adds emotional weight and credibility.

## B-roll as narrative fuel

B-roll isn't just decoration. It carries meaning. When you're building a story around a founder's journey, the right clip can show the stakes, the effort, or the turning point without needing words. This makes your message more visceral and memorable.

## Sourcing with intention

Don't settle for what's available. Actively seek out unique footage that aligns with your core message. Whether it's filming on location, repurposing raw clips from team meetings, or mining personal archives, the key is to prioritize originality over convenience. This approach builds a distinct visual identity and strengthens your brand.
