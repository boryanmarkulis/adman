# Caption and Hashtag Strategy

Including product names and relevant, specific hashtags in your captions boosts discoverability across platforms.

## Include the Product Name

Always mention the product by name in your caption, like "the hero product," to help users find your content through search. This small detail significantly improves visibility on platforms like YouTube where people search for specific terms.

## Use Relevant and Specific Hashtags

Pick hashtags that are both accurate and targeted, such as #hairtransformation or #hairextension. These help your content appear in searches and feed algorithms, especially when they match what real users are already looking for. Avoid generic tags; focus on ones that signal the actual topic of your video.
