# Caption style is about clarity and consistency

Good caption styling draws attention to key messages without distracting from the core content. It's not just about readability, but about reinforcing the message through visual rhythm.

## Use color and formatting strategically

Highlighting key phrases with bold and color changes helps guide the viewer's eye and emphasize meaning. Switching between colors like pink and white for specific lines creates visual punctuation that makes the message stick. This isn't decoration. It's a signal.

## Keep style consistent with voiceover tone

The caption design should mirror the rhythm and tone of the spoken word. If the voiceover is calm and deliberate, captions shouldn't jump around with random colors or fonts. Consistency builds trust and feels professional. A mismatch between audio and text can break immersion and confuse the viewer.

## Align with brand and content purpose

Strong caption style supports the overall message, whether it's educational, motivational, or promotional. It should match the brand voice and the emotional intent of the video, not stand out as an afterthought.
