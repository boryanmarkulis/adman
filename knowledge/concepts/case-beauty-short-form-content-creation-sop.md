# The Beauty Brand Short-Form Content Creation SOP

the beauty brand short-form content creation SOP is a repeatable, high-leverage system for producing scroll-stopping, conversion-driven videos using AI, precise visual design, and strategic CTAs.

## AI-Powered Script Generation

Use Chat GPT's Script Wizard to generate both captions and hook titles. For captions, paste this prompt:
"Write 10 video captions that are under 100 characters based on this script. Make them designed to get people to stop scrolling, watch the video till the end, and engage."

For hook titles, use:
"Write 10 hook titles that are under 7 words long for a viral video based on this video script that are designed to get people to stop scrolling and watch the video."

Always review and select the top 3 options from each batch. Don't settle for the first output.

## Visual Design Standards

All captions must use Montserrat font at 18px, with the first letter capitalized. Text color is white, with key phrases in Brand Pink and bolded. Add a black stroke at 69px for contrast and readability. Apply a "Bounce Out" animation to each caption line for visual pop.

## Visual Effects & Transitions

Enhance retention with strategic effects:
- Use b-roll footage during the hook if the original video quality is low.
- Include before/after visuals showing the impact of the hero product.
- Add teaser sections that hint at the payoff without giving it away.
- Apply smooth transitions between clips and sound effects to maintain energy.

## Strategic CTAs

Place clear, creative calls-to-action every 10 to 20 seconds. Show them 2 to 3 times per video. Examples:
- "B1G1 Free the hero product Linked Here!"
- "Add 2 hero products And Get 1 Free!"
- "B1G1 Running In Our Shop Right Now!"

CTAs must point directly to the shop icon and be visually distinct.

## Viral Research & Inspiration

Before creating content, search for viral videos in the hair niche using:
- TikTok
- Instagram
- YouTube Shorts
- Google

Use keywords like: *hair transformation, hair products, hair extensions, hair pieces, viral hair extensions, emotional hair*.

This research informs tone, pacing, and hook structure. Always reverse-engineer what worked in top-performing videos.
