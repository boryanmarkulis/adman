# The Hero Product Video Brief

the hero product video brief is a standardized content directive for creators, designed to ensure consistent, high-impact product storytelling that aligns with the beauty brand Extensions' brand identity and marketing goals.

## Core Requirements

Creators must film a 60-second or shorter video showing the unboxing and installation of the hero product® extension. The full video must be uploaded or submitted within seven days of receiving the product. This tight timeline ensures freshness and relevance in content distribution.

## Presentation Style

The brief mandates a clean, distraction-free format: no captions, no background music. This keeps the focus squarely on the product experience, the creator's natural voice, and the visual demonstration of ease and realism.

## Brand Messaging Anchors

Every video must incorporate specific keywords and messaging that reinforce core brand promises:
- **Color Match Guaranteed**  
- **Looks & Feels Like Real Hair**  
- **Completely Seamless Blend**  
- **Easy to Install**

These phrases are not optional. They serve as anchors for audience trust and are tied directly to the beauty brand Extensions' positioning around authenticity, quality, and user-friendliness.

## Strategic Purpose

This brief isn't just about content creation. It's a tool for scaling brand consistency across creators while reducing friction in content approval and distribution. By standardizing the format, tone, and key messages, the beauty brand ensures every video reinforces the same core value proposition, regardless of who is filming it.
