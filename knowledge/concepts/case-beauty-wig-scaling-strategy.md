# Wig Scaling Strategy

Wig Scaling is a content and product expansion playbook that combines creative repurposing, targeted outreach, and multi-platform content banking to grow a wig brand with precision and momentum.

## Core Components

Wig Scaling centers on three pillars: product innovation, ad evolution, and audience-specific content. It starts by launching new products and formalizing internal processes like the Wig DPA (Documented Product Agreement), which ensures consistency and scalability across launches.

Ads are not static. Winning creatives get remade into product detail page (PDP) assets to maximize conversion at every funnel stage. A single high-performing ad, such as one for menopause-related hair loss, is repurposed for new use cases like alopecia or other causes, extending its lifespan and reach.

## Creative Repurposing & Ad Expansion

The strategy embraces iterative refinement. Instead of starting from scratch, teams take proven ads and adapt them across formats: turning a video into a carousel, extracting key visuals for GIFs, or reworking copy for different audiences. This produces 65+ new GIF ads as part of the scaling cycle.

Before-and-after picture carousels are built to highlight transformation, leveraging emotional storytelling that resonates with users facing hair loss. These assets are optimized for both TikTok and Instagram, where visual proof drives trust and action.

## Audience-Specific Outreach

Wig Scaling targets underrepresented demographics through intentional outreach. Content creators and real users over 60 are invited to participate using hashtags like #womenover60 and #over60club. This builds authentic representation and expands reach into a high-intent, underserved market.
