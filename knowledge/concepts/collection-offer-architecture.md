# Collection Offer Architecture

## What It Is
Instead of betting on one hero product, build a collection of adjacent products under one category. The customer gets more choice, more reasons to buy again, and a better chance of finding a fit.

## Why It Works
- Raises AOV because people can bundle or add on
- Raises LTV because buyers can come back for different versions
- Catches more intent because not every customer wants the same exact product
- Makes seasonal pushes easier because the store has more than one thing to push

## When To Use It
It works best when, per the same screening in store skeletons:
- The market is mature enough that one product is getting stale
- Buyers already trust the brand
- You have enough traffic to test multiple offers
- You want to build a better Q4 or campaign structure

## How To Build It
- Keep the category tight
- Add proven winners, not random inventory
- Test bundles, mystery offers, and buy-more-save-more angles, judged the way [[product-test-selection-heuristics]] filters winners
- Use email, ads, and retargeting to learn which combinations convert
- Then push the winners harder
