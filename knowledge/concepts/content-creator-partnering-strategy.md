# Content Creator Partnering Strategy

A structured, daily review and selection process for identifying and onboarding high-quality content creators to align with brand goals.

## Daily Review & Selection System

The team runs a consistent daily workflow to evaluate potential content creators. Every day, 4 to 5 creator links are shared in a central channel. A Loom video is then created to document the review process, capturing thoughts, criteria, and decisions. This creates a transparent, auditable trail of how creators are assessed and selected.

## Purpose & Efficiency

This system ensures no promising talent slips through the cracks while maintaining consistency across selections. By reviewing creators daily and recording the rationale in Looms, the team builds institutional knowledge and reduces reliance on memory or ad-hoc decisions.

## Linking to Related Systems

This process integrates directly with [[content-creator-selection-criteria]] and [[content-creator-outreach-strategies]], ensuring that outreach efforts feed into a standardized evaluation pipeline. The documented reviews also support content creator performance tracking and content creator review process over time.
