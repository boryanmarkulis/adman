# Content Creator Selection Criteria

We prioritize creators who consistently deliver engaging short-form content in the hair niche, with a proven ability to drive strong view counts.

## Core Qualifications

The ideal creator demonstrates a track record of producing short-form videos that capture attention quickly and hold it. They don't just post content; they create material that performs well in terms of views, which signals audience resonance and platform favorability.

## Performance Over Price

While some creators may charge more, we're not prioritizing low cost over performance. A higher rate is acceptable if the creator delivers consistent results. The focus is on value, not just price.

## Niche Focus

We are specifically seeking talent within the hair content space. This includes creators who understand the nuances of hair care, styling, product use, and audience concerns relevant to that niche.

## Consistency Is Key

Inconsistent view performance is a red flag. We look for creators who can maintain a reliable output level and deliver measurable engagement over time, not just occasional viral moments.
