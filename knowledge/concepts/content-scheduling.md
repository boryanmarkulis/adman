# Content scheduling

Scheduling content is about syncing your creative output with a reliable publishing rhythm across platforms.

## Approval workflow before posting

Every piece of content must be cleared for approval before it goes live. This means checking that both the video and its caption have been reviewed and signed off. The process starts with verifying approvals in Google Drive, where all assets are stored and managed. Only after confirming these two elements should you proceed to publish. The most important thing before you even download the video is whether it was approved and whether the caption was approved.

## Platform-specific scheduling steps

On TikTok, once videos are downloaded from Google Drive, you can use the scheduler to set exact times for upload. This keeps your content pipeline smooth and consistent. For YouTube, you go into YouTube Studio, click on "Schedule," and pick a specific date and time. This allows you to plan ahead and maintain a steady stream of content without manual daily uploads.

## Central role of Google Drive

Google Drive acts as the central hub for all video assets. It's where files are stored, shared, and approved. Before any download or upload, the approval status must be confirmed. Once videos are downloaded and scheduled, you can close the Drive tabs to reduce clutter and stay focused.
