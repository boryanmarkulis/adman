# Content Sourcing Strategies

Sourcing content through customer participation and video asks unlocks authentic, high-engagement user-generated content that builds trust and amplifies reach.

## Customer-Driven Video Sourcing

Inviting customers to contribute videos by guiding them through a structured questionnaire turns passive users into active content creators. This method not only generates raw material for campaigns but also deepens customer investment in the brand. The key is simplicity: ask clear, focused questions that prompt storytelling, not performance.

## Integration with Content Systems

These user-generated clips can be fed directly into your content pipeline, where they serve as social proof, testimonials, or raw material for repurposing across platforms. When paired with a consistent review and editing process, customer videos become reliable assets in your content strategy.

## Strategic Benefits

- Builds community around shared experiences
- Reduces content creation burden while increasing authenticity
- Enhances credibility through real customer voices
- Fuels organic reach via social sharing and platform algorithms

This approach aligns closely with [[customer-voice]], [[ugc-content-system]], and [[content-creation-strategy]], and works best when combined with clear instructions and incentives. It's not just about collecting content, but creating a feedback loop where customers feel seen and valued.

## Best Practices

- Keep the ask simple and time-bound (under 3 minutes)
- Provide a script or question list to lower barriers
- Reward participation with early access, discounts, or recognition
- Always secure rights and permissions before use

This strategy is especially powerful in [[customer-insight-gathering]] and content outreach, where real stories outperform polished ads.
