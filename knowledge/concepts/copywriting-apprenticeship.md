# Copywriting Apprenticeship

## What It Means
Copywriting is a reps game. You do not get good by talking about good copy. You get good by writing, reading, revising, and getting corrected until the pattern recognition shows up.

The first useful role is apprentice, not authority. That means lots of practice, lots of feedback, and a short leash on anything that matters to revenue.

## The Training Stack
- Read strong copy every day.
- Write ad copy, headlines, landing pages, scripts, and blog posts.
- Rewrite the same angle in different forms until the structure is obvious.
- Compare drafts against real response, not vibes, the same standard [[finding-winning-copy-through-testing]] runs on.
- Get direct feedback from someone who knows what good looks like.

## Why It Matters
Needle-moving work is too important to delegate blindly. If the founder cannot spot a weak hook, a fake story, or a broken CTA, the team will just make expensive noise.

The goal is not to make someone sound smart. The goal is to make them useful.

## What To Practice
Practice the stuff that actually moves money:
- believable story flow
- funnel logic
- specificity
- direct CTA
- matching the buyer
- objection handling
- finding holes in the business and writing them down clearly

## Operating Rule
If the writing does not lead the reader somewhere, it is not finished, and it starts with [[customer-research-before-copy]].

If a role can be executed by someone with no copy judgment, it is not yet the highest-value version of that role.
