# Creator Outreach Assistant Handbook

The Creator Outreach Assistant is the backbone of scalable, consistent UGC and influencer outreach, handling everything from list building to content tracking and delivery.

## Core Responsibilities

The Assistant owns the full lifecycle of creator outreach. This starts with identifying and curating qualified creators and influencers through targeted list-building. They then send personalized initial outreach emails, ensuring tone and messaging align with brand voice and campaign goals.

They manage all logistics tied to content creation, including creating draft orders in Shopify for each selected creator. It's critical that these orders are set up correctly: only single hero products should be sent, not 2-packs, to maintain consistency in content production and tracking.

## Daily Operations & Reporting

Every day, the Assistant sends a concise end-of-day report to the Content Manager via Slack. This includes key metrics like number of outreach messages sent, draft orders created, and status updates on content submissions. These reports keep the team aligned and allow for quick adjustments to strategy based on real-time data.

## System Integration

This role sits at the intersection of content creation, logistics, and communication. The Assistant works closely with the Content Manager and supports broader systems like the UGC creator agreement process, content-creator-partnering-strategy, and content-creation-process. Their work feeds directly into the larger content-outreach system and helps maintain rhythm in the solar-outreach-and-pipeline-rhythm.

## Key Principles

- Accuracy over speed: Getting SKUs right and outreach lists clean matters more than hitting arbitrary numbers.
- Consistency: Daily reporting builds trust and visibility across the team.
- Proactivity: Anticipating bottlenecks in shipping or creator response helps prevent delays in content delivery.

This role is not just about sending emails. It's about creating a reliable, repeatable engine for scaling authentic creator partnerships.
