# Creator Outreach Email Templates

The beauty brand Extensions uses a structured email outreach system to engage content creators, offering free product in exchange for authentic short-form video reviews.

## Template Structure and Offer

The core of the outreach is a simple, consistent email that invites creators to receive two the hero product at no cost. The message includes a direct link to the product page so they can choose their preferred styles. This low-friction entry point increases response rates by removing decision fatigue and making participation feel effortless.

## Follow-Up Sequence

A follow-up email is sent 48 hours after the initial outreach to re-engage creators who haven't responded. The tone is warm and personal, referencing the creator's content to show genuine interest. This small nudge helps maintain momentum without feeling pushy.

## Content Guidance and Brand Alignment

Each outreach includes a clear video brief that outlines how to film the review. The guide covers key points like shot composition, lighting, and messaging to ensure content aligns with brand values. It also links to sample videos for reference, helping creators understand the expected quality and style.

## Asset Management Workflow

Creators are given a dedicated Google Drive folder to upload their raw footage and before/after photos. This system keeps all assets organized and traceable, making it easy to track submissions and streamline the review process. It also reduces back-and-forth by setting clear expectations for file delivery.
