# Customer UGC Content Instructions

Customer-generated content for the hero product must meet clear visual and narrative standards to ensure authenticity, quality, and marketing effectiveness.

## Photo Requirements

For photo submissions, customers must provide three high-quality images within seven days of accepting the offer:
- One image showing the customer before putting in the hero product
- One from the front after installation
- One from behind after installation
All photos should be taken with natural lighting or a ring light, and filmed in front of a clean, aesthetically pleasing background such as a vanity desk or bathroom setup.

## Video Requirements

For video submissions, customers must record a 1-3 minute video that includes:
- A clear demonstration of the hero product installation process
- Honest commentary on their experience while putting it on
- A direct call to action encouraging viewers to visit [client domain removed]
The video should follow the example provided in the official tutorial.

## Lighting and Setup

Use natural light or a ring light for optimal clarity. Avoid harsh shadows or overexposure. Film in front of a tidy, visually appealing background like a vanity or bathroom area to maintain brand consistency and professionalism.

## Tutorial Reference

Customers are directed to an official installation guide at [https://youtu.be/Gm56ygBSa-s](https://youtu.be/Gm56ygBSa-s) to ensure proper setup. While this is the recommended resource, customers may also use other trusted tutorials for reference.

## Purpose and Quality

These instructions exist to maintain a consistent, authentic visual narrative across customer UGC. High-quality content not only builds trust but also strengthens social proof in marketing efforts. This standard supports both the [[content-creation-process]] and [[ugc-content-system]] by ensuring all submissions meet baseline quality and storytelling criteria.
