# Customer Voice

Your content should speak the language your customer already uses and consumes, not the one you wish they would.

## Match Their Media Habits

People don't switch their media habits to fit your brand. If your customers are on Instagram, TikTok, or YouTube Shorts, that's where you need to show up. Don't force them into a format they ignore just because it's easier for you to produce. Make content your customer already consumes.

## Use Their Words, Not Yours

The phrases and tone your customers use in reviews, comments, or social posts are the real language of your audience. Pull those expressions into your content. It builds trust faster than any polished brand voice ever could.

## Learn from What They Already Engage With

Look at the content they share, like, or comment on. That's not just engagement data. It's a signal of what resonates. Repurpose and refine that material instead of starting from scratch.

## Connect Through Shared Context

When your content mirrors the way your customer talks about their problems and wins, it feels familiar. That familiarity builds connection. You're not selling to them. You're speaking with them.

## Respect Their Diverse Realities

People's reactions to solar outreach vary widely, ranging from strong opposition to existing adoption, indicating diverse personal beliefs and experiences. Some see solar as a financial lifeline; others view it as a political statement or a disruption. There is no single "customer" profile. Your voice must reflect this range, not flatten it.
