# Editing Strategy

Editing isn't just about trimming clips or fixing audio. It's a strategic tool to shape emotion, control pacing, and keep people watching.

## Strategic Cuts and Pauses

The timing between cuts and the deliberate use of silence aren't accidents. They're designed to create tension, highlight key moments, and trigger emotional reactions. A well-placed pause can make a viewer lean in. A sudden cut at the right moment can surprise or emphasize meaning.

This isn't about following rules blindly. It's about using rhythm and timing to guide attention and feeling. The goal is to keep people engaged by playing with their expectations.

## Emotional Momentum

Good editing builds momentum. Short, sharp cuts can create urgency. Longer pauses can build anticipation. When used intentionally, these shifts in pacing help tell a story that feels alive and human.

This approach works best when it aligns with the core message and emotional arc of the content. It's not just about style. It's about making the viewer feel something.
