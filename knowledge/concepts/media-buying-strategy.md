# Media Buying Strategy

Media buying is about maximizing returns by doubling down on what works, not chasing novelty.

## Focus on Winning Ads

The most effective media buying doesn't rely on constant new content. Instead, it pushes winning ads to their limits by testing them across different audiences, CBOs (Campaign Budget Optimization), and scaling budgets. The goal is to squeeze every dollar of performance from proven creatives rather than burn through time and money on untested variations.

## Test with Purpose

On TikTok, the strategy centers around video views as the primary metric. Run one campaign with your best ABO (Ad Breakout Optimization) or ACO (Ad Creative Optimization) ads at a $20 per-result cost threshold. This forces focus on performance and helps identify which creative formats deliver real engagement at scale.

## Structure for Scale

The beauty brand Extensions uses a system of multiple CBOs, broad targeting with automatic rules, and full creative rotation across campaigns. The process includes duplicating collection pages to send traffic, testing all creatives in parallel, and iterating based on top performers. This structure supports rapid scaling while maintaining brand visibility and awareness through video reviews and consistent messaging.

## Avoid Constant Creation

Creating new content for every test cycle is inefficient. Instead, the strategy prioritizes deep testing of existing winners across formats, audiences, and delivery mechanisms. This reduces fatigue, improves ROI, and builds momentum from proven assets.
