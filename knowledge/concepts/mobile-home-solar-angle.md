# Mobile Home Solar Angle

Reframing solar access for mobile homeowners as a fairness issue creates powerful emotional resonance and conversion lift.

## The Fairness Frame

Mobile home owners pay the same utility rates as homeowners in larger homes, yet were historically excluded from solar access. This disparity becomes a compelling narrative when framed as systemic inequity. The core message highlights that neighbors with bigger properties enjoy solar benefits while mobile homeowners are left behind despite equal financial burden.

## The "They Told You No" Rejection Flip

The most effective version acknowledges past denial directly: installers said no, lenders refused, door-knockers skipped the street. This validates the customer's experience and builds trust. By stating that qualification has changed, it turns rejection into a story of progress and access. The message isn't just "you can get solar now" but "they were wrong before, and you're not being denied anymore."

## Why It Works

This angle taps into two deep truths: the frustration of being excluded despite equal footing, and the relief of finally being seen as eligible. It bypasses skepticism by admitting past failures instead of pretending they never happened. The result is a high-trust, high-conversion message that aligns with [[customer-insight]] and truth and perspective.
