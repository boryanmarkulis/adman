# Offer Page Design

A well-designed offer page aligns brand identity, user experience, and conversion goals through intentional layout, testing, and iteration.

## Strategic Element Placement

The positioning of core elements like the logo, promotions, and free gifts isn't arbitrary. Moving them strategically can reinforce brand presence and guide user attention. For example, returning the logo to the top of the page helps anchor the brand early in the user journey, creating a stronger sense of trust and consistency. This kind of repositioning is often driven by real-time feedback and testing rather than default templates.

## Testing Promotional Elements

Promotions aren't just add-ons; they're conversion levers. Testing variations like mystery gifts or free shipping thresholds helps uncover what resonates most with your audience. The goal isn't to guess what works, but to experiment with different psychological triggers. For instance, a "mystery gift" creates curiosity and perceived value, while a clear free shipping threshold sets expectations and reduces friction.

## Optimizing Pricing and Promotions

The highest revenue doesn't always come from the lowest price or most generous offer. Instead, it emerges from testing combinations of pricing tiers, discount triggers, and promotional messaging. Data shows that certain thresholds, like a "free shipping" bar, can outperform others in driving conversions. These insights come not from assumptions but from live A/B tests that measure actual user behavior.
