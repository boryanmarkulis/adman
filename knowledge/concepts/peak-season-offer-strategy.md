# BFCM 2024 Offer Strategy

BFCM 2024 centers on layered, high-value bundles and time-sensitive promotions to drive urgency and conversion across multiple product lines.

## Core Offer Structure

The campaign features a mix of standalone bundles and dynamic combo offers. Key offerings include:
- The **Dirty Blonde Extension Bundle** at $99, combining core products for a curated experience.
- A **B2G1 Free Hair Pieces** deal, where customers get one free piece with every purchase.
- **Buy 1 Get 1 Free hero products**, doubling value on the hero product.
- **3 For $99.95 Wigs**, offering a low-cost entry point to full wigs.

## Strategic Bundles and Add-Ons

Several high-impact bundles are designed to increase average order value:
- **HF Wand + the hero product + Serum** combines the hair wand, extension, and serum for a complete styling system.
- **Wand + Oil + Bonnet** creates a care-and-styling kit ideal for new users.
- The **Dirty Blonde the hero product + Ponytail + Messy Bun + Natural Hair Dye** bundle targets customers seeking a full transformation with natural-looking results.

## Content and Creative Execution

The campaign leverages multiple formats to showcase offers:
- **Images** highlight product combinations and lifestyle use.
- **Videos** demonstrate real-world application, especially for complex bundles.
- **Carousels** walk users through the value of each offer in sequence, emphasizing savings and convenience.

## Targeted Listicle Offer

A **BFCM Listicle of best products for moms 35+** is used to position the brand as a trusted guide. This list includes the Dirty Blonde the hero product bundle and other high-performing items, reinforcing relevance and personalization.

## Key Psychological Triggers

The strategy uses urgency through:
- Time-limited flash sales.
- Limited inventory messaging on key bundles.
- Tiered value stacking (e.g., B2G1 + % off sitewide) to amplify perceived savings.

This approach blends product bundling, audience-specific curation, and multi-format content to maximize conversion during the BFCM window.
