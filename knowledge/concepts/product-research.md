# Product research is about spotting early trends with real momentum

The best product ideas come not from spreadsheets or market reports, but from watching what people are actually engaging with right now.

## Watch the For Your Page (FYP) like a hawk

TikTok's FYP isn't just entertainment. It's a live feed of emerging demand. The most reliable signal for a viable product isn't data from a year ago or a niche forum. It's what's trending *today* in videos with high engagement, comments, and shares. If something keeps showing up across creators, especially in the same niche, it's worth investigating.

## Look for three things: content, margins, and balance

A good product to test has three non-negotiables:
- **Existing creatives**: There should be tons of videos already made about it. That means people know what it is, how to use it, and why they want it.
- **Healthy margins**: You need room to scale without getting crushed by cost or ad spend. Avoid anything with razor-thin profit margins.
- **Not oversaturated**: Just because a product has content doesn't mean you should jump in. If every other shop is selling it, you're just another drop in the ocean. Look for products where the trend is rising but not yet flooded.

## Act early, stay sharp

The window to own a trend is narrow. The moment everyone starts copying it, the game changes. Your job isn't to be first in the world, but first in your audience's mind. Use the FYP to spot patterns before they go mainstream, then test fast with low risk.
