# Product Testing SOP

A repeatable process for testing new product ideas through curated video content and performance tracking.

## Core Workflow

The SOP starts with curating and uploading videos that showcase specific products. Each video must include a clear caption, be tagged with the correct product, and be published through Instagram Shop to enable direct monetization. This setup turns every piece of content into a testable sales channel.

## Testing Scope

You don't just test one product at a time. The SOP requires testing multiple product types simultaneously, like hair toppers, extensions, and wellness tools such as air massagers. Each product gets its own dedicated set of videos and unique product tags to isolate performance data and avoid cross-contamination in results.

## Performance Tracking

Success is measured using Commerce Manager insights. You track which videos generate actual sales, not just views or likes. This real-time feedback loop lets you quickly identify what content resonates with your audience and drives conversions, making it easier to double down on winning formats.

## Integration with Other Systems

This SOP ties directly into broader marketing systems like Instagram Shop and the Shopify Commerce Manager. It also supports larger strategies such as organic monetization, product-market fit testing, and content repurposing across platforms.

## Origin and Documentation

The process was formalized from a recorded operating walkthrough. Keep the working SOP version-controlled and refine it from actual performance data. It is part of a larger testing system that includes [[ad-testing-strategy]] and [[product-market-fit-testing]].
