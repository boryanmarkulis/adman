# The ROI Calculator Advertorial Strategy

This strategy uses timely, emotionally charged news hooks to drive engagement and conversions through a structured advertorial funnel that prioritizes ROI framing and native Facebook interaction.

## Core Structure of the Advertorial

Every post follows a reusable play structure:
- **News hook**: A real-time energy crisis or blackout story from the local market or similar markets.
- **Power-cost bridge**: Connects the event to local electricity costs and the financial burden on expat villa owners.
- **ROI reframe**: Positions solar not as a reliability fix, but as a smart investment with clear payback timelines.
- **Calculator CTA**: Directs users to comment "solar" instead of clicking external links.

This format has proven effective across multiple rounds and is now documented for consistent reuse.

## Why "Comment Solar" Beats External Links

Using Facebook's native comment-to-DM automation outperforms external link clicks in conversion. The "comment solar" CTA avoids link throttling, leverages platform-native engagement, and drives higher-quality leads through automated follow-up. This has been validated across Rounds 1 to 5 of the funnel.

## Proactive Deployment Based on Real-Time Events

Instead of posting about outdated news, the strategy pre-builds advertorials in advance and deploys them same-day when a new viral blackout or energy crisis breaks. This ensures freshness, relevance, and maximum virality potential.

A monitoring brief runs 24/7 on a server, scanning for high-impact the local market energy stories. When a story meets thresholds for scale and timeliness, Sol pings the operator via WhatsApp to trigger immediate deployment.

## Focus on ROI Framing Over Reliability

Audience research shows that expat villa owners respond more strongly to investment-focused messaging than blackout or reliability claims. Framing solar as a financial decision with clear payback timelines drives higher engagement and conversion rates.

## Funnel Mechanics and Tracking

The full funnel is built around niche-local Facebook groups, comment CTAs, and automated DM delivery. Results are tracked round-by-round in `results.md`, enabling rapid iteration and optimization. The goal is to maximize conversions while minimizing friction and platform throttling.

This system turns reactive news into a scalable, automated lead generation engine.
