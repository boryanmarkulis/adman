# Short-Form Mastery System

A structured program for scaling short-form video output across multiple platforms through consistent daily posting, batch production, and a small two-person team.

## The Core Idea

Posting 2 to 4 short-form videos per day across 7 platforms generates 420 to 840 total posts per month. This volume, combined with consistency, dramatically increases your odds of going viral. The system works because more platforms mean more reach and more chances for content to resonate with different audiences. Skipping days or limiting yourself to just one or two platforms kills momentum and caps your growth.

## Three Pillars

Everything in the system boils down to three non-negotiables:

1. **Production.** Film in batches using a dedicated studio setup. This removes friction and lets you produce 120 videos in about 10 hours when prepared properly.
2. **Editing.** Process raw footage into polished clips. See [[short-form-video-editing]] for the workflow.
3. **Distribution.** Schedule and post across platforms. Use system assets like a scheduler, content library, and tracker to keep things running smoothly. See [[short-form-content-scheduling]].

## Team Structure

You can run this alone or bring in up to two people: one editor and one social media manager, often the same person handling both roles. The creator focuses only on filming, while the team handles editing and distribution. If someone needs to be removed from the system, share their name, email, and position.

## Week 1 Setup

Before you film a single video, lock down three essentials:

- **Brand identity worksheet.** This is your north star. Don't rush it. It guides every piece of content and ensures consistency. See brand identity worksheet.
- **System assets.** Set up your scheduler, content library, and tracker in the distribution space. These keep the machine running without reinventing the wheel each week.
- **Review process.** When giving feedback, always pair critique with genuine positive reinforcement. It builds better morale, stronger momentum, and a healthier team culture.
