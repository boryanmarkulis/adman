# Short-form video hook creation

A strong hook is the foundation of any high-performing short-form video, setting the tone and capturing attention in under three seconds.

## The first three seconds matter most

The opening moments of a video are make-or-break. If viewers aren't hooked immediately, they'll scroll away. This is where you either win them over or lose them entirely. Every second counts, and the first three seconds must deliver a punch that stops the scroll.

## Use shock, surprise, or curiosity to hook

The most effective hooks often challenge assumptions or reveal something unexpected. For example, a video that exposes how affordable human hair extensions are sourced from city drains immediately grabs attention with a shocking truth. This kind of reveal triggers curiosity and compels viewers to keep watching to understand the full story.

## Reuse content with fresh hooks for longer reach

You don't need to create new content every time. Repurposing existing videos with updated hooks, captions, or visuals can breathe new life into old material. Posting a video again on a different platform with a new angle or framing can unlock entirely new audiences and performance.

## Maintain brand identity through visual consistency

Even when experimenting with new styles, keep your brand visible. Use branded colors and fonts in your captions to reinforce recognition. For example, using your brand's signature yellow or pink in text overlays helps maintain consistency across platforms while still allowing creative freedom in the visuals.
