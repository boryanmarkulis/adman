# Short-form video script bank

A short-form video script bank is a curated collection of reusable, high-performing video scripts tailored to specific content types and audience triggers in the hair extension space.

## Core formats and structures

Scripts for hair tips often follow a clear, repeatable structure: start with a 6- to 9-second b-roll clip showing the problem (like thinning hair), overlay a title card with a compelling question or list ("6 reasons why your hair might be thinning"), then deliver the content as a concise list using emojis and short sentences. This format leverages visual storytelling and cognitive ease, making it ideal for platforms like TikTok and Instagram Reels.

## Problem-to-solution framing

The most effective scripts begin with a relatable pain point shown visually, not just described. A woman with visibly thinning hair in the opening frame immediately grounds the viewer in the experience. This is followed by a title card that names the issue directly, creating instant relevance and emotional resonance before delivering actionable insights.

## Story-driven hooks

Reddit-style scripts use narrative tension to hook viewers. They open with a provocative question ("Reddit, what is your worst experience getting a haircut?"), then tell a short, dramatic personal story (e.g., being cut too short by a stylist). The story builds empathy and curiosity, leading naturally into a product pitch for the hero product as a solution to avoid such mishaps.

## Emphasis on visual and textual consistency

Every script in the bank maintains strong visual alignment: consistent font choices, stroke effects on text, and b-roll that matches the topic. This creates brand recognition and improves retention. The use of emojis and short phrases keeps content digestible and aligns with platform algorithms favoring fast-paced, scannable formats.

## Integration with broader systems

These scripts are not standalone. They feed into the [[short-form-content-production-system]], support the hero product video content strategy, and inform the [[ad-script-flow]]. When paired with optimized b roll sources and [[caption-style-consistency]], they form a repeatable engine for content creation that scales without sacrificing authenticity.

## Why it works

This system works because it combines emotional storytelling, visual proof of the problem, and clear solutions in under 30 seconds. It respects attention spans while building trust through real-life scenarios and transparent value delivery.
