# Short-form video

Short-form video is a powerful tool for capturing attention and driving sales by delivering high-impact messages in under 60 seconds.

## Core Purpose
Short-form video excels at hooking viewers quickly and guiding them toward a conversion. The format thrives on immediacy, emotional resonance, and clarity, making it ideal for product discovery and lead generation. A well-crafted short can turn passive scrollers into engaged prospects by delivering value or intrigue in the first few seconds.

## Strategic Use
The most effective short-form videos are not just content they're conversion engines. They're designed to align with specific offers and funnel stages, using proven hooks and structures that drive action. Success comes from pairing the right video with the right product, audience, and call-to-action.

## Integration with Other Systems
Short-form video works best when embedded in a larger system. It connects directly with content strategy, ad creative, and sales funnels. When combined with tools like form automation, CRM integration, and performance tracking, it becomes part of a scalable growth engine rather than isolated content.

## Key Considerations
- Focus on the first 3 seconds: if you don't hook, you lose.
- Prioritize clarity over cleverness; your message must land fast.
- Reuse and refine top-performing hooks across multiple videos to maximize efficiency.
- Track performance not just by views, but by conversion impact.
