# Testing is Iterative

Testing isn't a one-off event. It's a continuous loop where every experiment teaches you something that shapes the next one.

## Learn from Every Test

Don't just run a test and move on. After each round, review the data: what drove CTR, what spiked CPM, what flopped. Write down the insights, especially the surprises. This reflection turns raw numbers into real learning.

## Refine Through Feedback Loops

Use what you learn to tweak your next test. Adjust copy, visuals, targeting, or format based on performance. Each iteration should be smarter than the last, not just different.

## Metrics Guide the Process

Focus on key metrics like CTR and CPM to measure what's working. But don't just chase numbers. Ask why a variation performed well. Was it the headline? The image angle? The timing? Let the data point you toward deeper understanding.

## Connect Testing to Strategy

Testing isn't isolated. It feeds directly into your broader ad-creative-testing-strategy and ad-iteration-strategy. The insights from each test help refine your approach across campaigns, formats, and audiences.

## Build a Culture of Iteration

Treat every test as a chance to improve, not just a step toward a goal. This mindset turns trial and error into a reliable engine for growth. It's how you turn random experiments into predictable progress.
