# TikTok Ad Strategy

TikTok ads work best when they feel native, authentic, and hyper-relevant to a specific audience.

## Niche Targeting Over Broad Demographics

The most effective TikTok campaigns don't aim for everyone. Instead, they zero in on a tight niche and speak directly to that group's interests, language, and pain points. The creative itself becomes the targeting mechanism. Ads that resonate with a defined community outperform those trying to appeal to a wider, less engaged audience.

## Optimal Ad Length for Conversion

Ads between 21 and 34 seconds consistently deliver the highest conversion lift, averaging a 280% increase in performance compared to shorter or longer formats. This window gives enough time to build context, show product use, and drive action without losing attention.

## Leverage Trends and Recency

TikTok's algorithm rewards freshness. Ads that tap into current trends, sounds, or cultural moments are more likely to be surfaced and engaged with. Staying on top of what's trending isn't just helpful; it's a performance lever.

## Use the Creative Center for Benchmarking

The TikTok Creative Center is a goldmine. It shows you real-time data on the top-performing ads across categories, filtered by engagement, reach, 6-second view rate, and more. Use it to reverse-engineer what works in your niche and test ideas that already have proven traction.

## Core Elements of High-Performing Ads

Every winning TikTok ad includes:
- A strong, immediate hook
- Clear, repeated product shots
- A human face (no stock imagery)
- On-screen text for silent viewing
- Background sound (not just music)
- Natural, non-salesy tone
- User-generated content (UGC) to build trust
- A direct, visible call to action

These aren't optional extras. They're the foundation of TikTok ad success.
