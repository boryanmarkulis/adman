# TikTok Best Practices

TikTok best practices center on leveraging platform-specific insights and maximizing content reuse across channels for efficient growth.

## Post Videos Longer Than 1 Minute

TikTok's analytics tools, like the Insights tab, now recommend posting videos that exceed one minute in length. This shift aligns with the platform's expanded video limits, which now allow up to 30 minutes on the main feed. Longer content performs better because it keeps users engaged and signals quality to the algorithm. Use this to your advantage by crafting informative or emotionally resonant videos that naturally extend beyond the typical short-form limit.

## Repurpose Content Across Platforms with Minimal Editing

High-performing TikTok videos can be directly repurposed for other platforms without heavy remixing. If a video gains traction on TikTok, post it on Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts, or even LinkedIn with minimal adjustments. The core message and visual hook often transfer well across audiences. This reduces production overhead and amplifies reach by leveraging proven content.
