# TikTok Shop is a high-margin e-commerce play

TikTok Shop lets you sell products with strong profit margins by leveraging short-form video content and built-in discovery.

## High profit potential per sale

You can achieve at least $6 in profit per item sold on TikTok Shop, even when pricing products at just $12. The platform's low customer acquisition cost and high conversion rates from engaging content make this margin sustainable without needing massive volume.

## Built for viral product discovery

TikTok Shop integrates directly with the app's algorithm-driven feed, so your products get discovered through organic engagement rather than paid ads alone. This reduces reliance on expensive ad spend while still driving sales.

## Fast iteration and testing

The combination of TikTok's content engine and Shop's tagging system allows you to test product ideas rapidly. You can link a new product in a video, track performance in real time, and double down on what works without long setup cycles.
