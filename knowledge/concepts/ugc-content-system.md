# UGC Content System

A customer-driven content pipeline that turns real product experiences into branded, paid-for social proof.

## Submission Process

Customers submit their user-generated content (UGC) directly via email. The system treats different content types differently, with payment structured based on whether the submission is a picture or a video. This encourages participation while maintaining clear value exchange.

## Content Organization

All customer submissions are organized using a standardized folder structure within Google Drive. Separate folders are maintained for pictures and videos to ensure easy retrieval, categorization, and workflow efficiency. This structure supports both immediate use and long-term content reuse across campaigns.

## Tracking & Attribution

A simple form collects key metadata with every submission, including where the customer first discovered the product. This helps trace marketing effectiveness and refine outreach strategies over time. It also adds context to each piece of UGC when repurposed.

## Payment Workflow

A dedicated tracking section ensures creators are paid promptly after content approval. Payments are logged and processed based on content type and agreed terms, with clear visibility into what's pending or completed. This builds trust and incentivizes consistent participation.
