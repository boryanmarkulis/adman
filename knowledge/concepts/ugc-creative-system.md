# UGC Creative System

A structured, daily workflow for generating and iterating on user-generated content ads at scale.

## Daily Output Target

The system is built around producing a minimum of five new or refined ads every day. This includes three brand-new creative concepts and two iterations of existing ads, maintaining a consistent 60/40 ratio between new content and refinement. This balance ensures continuous innovation while reinforcing what works.

## Core Roles & Responsibilities

The system relies on three interlocking roles: creator management, creative strategy, and video editing. Each role is accountable for specific stages of the process, from sourcing authentic creator input to finalizing polished, high-performing video assets. Clear ownership prevents bottlenecks and keeps momentum steady.

## Integration with Broader Systems

This system connects directly with [[ugc-content-system]], [[ad-creative-testing-loop]], and creative iteration strategy, ensuring that every output feeds into testing, learning, and scaling loops. It also supports [[regenerative-advertising]] by prioritizing authentic, reusable content that builds long-term brand trust.

## Scalability & Discipline

The 5-ad minimum is not a suggestion but a daily discipline. As volume increases, the 60/40 ratio remains fixed, preserving creative freshness while optimizing for proven performance. This rhythm prevents stagnation and supports sustainable growth in competitive markets.
