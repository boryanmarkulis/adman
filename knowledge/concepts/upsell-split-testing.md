# Upsell Split Testing

Upsell split testing is a method to optimize revenue by comparing different pricing strategies for upsells, aiming to increase average order value without sacrificing conversion rates.

## What It Tests

It focuses on evaluating how changes in price points affect customer behavior. The core idea is simple: test whether raising the price of an upsell (e.g., from $39.95 to $59.95) leads to higher revenue per customer while keeping conversion rates stable or improving.

## How It Works

You run two versions of the same upsell offer, differing only in price. One version uses the current price; the other tests a higher price point. You track conversion rates and total revenue generated per customer across both variants. The goal is to find the price that maximizes revenue without deterring buyers.

## Why It Matters

Pricing isn't just about cost or value perception. It's a lever for growth. A small increase in price, if conversion holds, directly boosts profit margins and unit economics. This testing approach helps you avoid guessing and instead make data-driven decisions about where to position your upsell.
