# Video content optimization

Optimizing video content means refining your videos to maximize revenue, especially through affiliate commissions by focusing on high-RPM topics and tracking performance.

## Revenue per Mille (RPM) as a Key Metric

Tracking RPM is central to video content optimization. High RPM indicates videos that generate more income per thousand views, which directly impacts affiliate earnings. You need to check analytics regularly to identify top-performing videos and understand what drives higher revenue. Without access to analytics, you can't maximize your commission, so logging in is non-negotiable.

## Focus on High-Value Products

Videos centered around high-value products like hair care and beauty items tend to pull in higher RPMs. This is because these niches often have stronger affiliate commission rates. Prioritizing content around such products increases the likelihood of earning more per view. The data shows a clear pattern: videos discussing these products consistently deliver better revenue signals.

## Precision in Visual Execution

Optimization isn't just about topic or analytics. It extends to the fine details of production. For example, caption spacing must be tight and precise, matching proven winning formats like the target region ad. Adjusting card shrink, removing excessive padding, and preserving word boundaries during animation pops ensures consistency with high-performing templates. Small visual tweaks can make a measurable difference in retention and perceived professionalism.

## Data-Driven Content Refinement

Use performance data from analytics to refine your video strategy. Look at which topics, formats, and product mentions lead to better RPMs, then double down on those patterns. This isn't guesswork; it's iteration based on real revenue signals. Every edit should be tied back to a metric that matters.
