# The Viral Checklist

Before you hit publish on a short-form video, run it through the Viral Checklist to dramatically increase its odds of going viral.

## The 7 Questions

The checklist is built around seven core questions that test whether your video has the ingredients most likely to trigger virality. Answering "yes" to all seven doesn't guarantee fame, but it means you're working with a proven framework that aligns with what actually spreads.

1. **Does it have a viral hook?**  
   The first three seconds must stop the scroll. It should be surprising, emotionally charged, or instantly intriguing. If it doesn't grab attention immediately, nothing else matters.

2. **Is the cutting viral?**  
   Every cut should serve momentum. No dead air, no filler. Transitions are sharp and purposeful. The rhythm keeps viewers locked in, not just watching but *anticipating* what comes next.

3. **Are the subtitles optimized for virality?**  
   Subtitles aren't just for sound-off viewing. They're a core part of the hook and message delivery. They must be bold, concise, and timed perfectly to land punchlines or key insights.

4. **Does it make sense without sound?**  
   80% of viewers watch muted. If your video relies on audio cues or voiceover for meaning, it fails this test. The visuals, text, and pacing must tell the full story alone.

5. **Is there a clear before-and-after transformation?**  
   Viewers share what changes them. Whether it's a mindset shift, a skill learned, or a problem solved, your video should show a tangible difference from start to finish.

6. **Does it trigger emotion or curiosity?**  
   Viral content doesn't just inform. It makes people feel something. Either they're shocked, inspired, amused, or left wondering "what happens next?"

7. **Is the message simple and repeatable?**  
   The core idea should be easy to remember and share. If someone can't summarize it in one sentence, you've lost the viral edge.

## Why It Works

This checklist isn't guesswork. It's distilled from patterns seen across thousands of high-performing short-form videos. It forces creators to audit their content not just for quality, but for *shareability*. The more you align with these structural and emotional triggers, the better your video performs in algorithmic feeds.

## How to Use It

Use this checklist as a final gate before posting. Run every video through it. Even if you're confident, the act of answering each question sharpens your instinct. Over time, you'll start building videos that *feel* viral before they even go live.
