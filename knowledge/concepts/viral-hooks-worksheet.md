# The Viral Hooks Worksheet

A ready-to-use template bank that gives creators instant access to proven hook structures for short-form video content.

## What It Is

The Viral Hooks Worksheet is a curated collection of over 90 to 100+ hook templates organized across nine distinct categories. These aren't just random lines; they're battle-tested frameworks designed to grab attention in the first two seconds of a video. Each template is built around high-engagement patterns that work across platforms like TikTok, Instagram Reels, and YouTube Shorts.

## How It Works

Instead of staring at a blank screen trying to invent something fresh every time, you plug into existing structures that already know how to hook viewers. The worksheet breaks down the anatomy of viral openings into categories like curiosity gaps, shock statements, personal stories, data reveals, and problem-agitation. You pick a category that fits your content angle, then fill in the blanks with your unique message.

## Why It Matters

Hooks are the gatekeepers of attention. If your first line doesn't land, your video dies before it starts. This worksheet removes the guesswork. It gives you a reliable starting point every time, so you can focus on delivery and value instead of scrambling for an opener. Used consistently, it becomes part of your content creation system, boosting retention and increasing the odds your videos get shared.
