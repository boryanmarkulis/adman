# Viral Hooks

A viral hook is the first five seconds of a video that grabs attention and compels viewers to keep watching.

## The 5-Second Rule

The first five seconds are make-or-break. If your hook doesn't land, most viewers will scroll away. A strong hook isn't just flashy or loud; it's a precise, high-impact statement or moment that creates curiosity, urgency, or emotional resonance. It sets the tone and promises value fast.

## What Makes a Hook Viral-Worthy

It must disrupt expectations. Use contradiction, surprise, or a bold claim to stop the scroll. The best hooks don't just say *something* interesting. They make the viewer feel they *must* see what comes next. This is where storytelling, timing, and psychological triggers align.

## Hook Types That Work

- **Contradiction**: "This is the worst thing you can do for your solar savings."
- **Urgency**: "You're losing $300 a month on your energy bill right now."
- **Curiosity Gap**: "I didn't think this would work... until I tried it."
- **Relatability with a Twist**: "I used to hate my electricity bill. Then I did this one thing."
