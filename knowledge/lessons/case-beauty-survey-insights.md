# The Beauty Brand Survey Insights

## The Pattern
The survey is blunt: people are not buying hair. They are buying the feeling of having their hair back under control. The repeat theme is confidence, length, volume, and looking more put together fast.

Thin hair, balding spots, postpartum shedding, menopause changes, stress, illness, thyroid issues, and age show up over and over. The product is a fast emotional fix for a physical change they did not choose.

## What Blocks Purchase
Color match is the biggest gate. If they do not trust the shade, they do not buy or they buy and never wear it.

The next blockers are quality, fit, price, and whether it will look fake. People worry about small heads, loose bands, heavy pieces, visible wires, and synthetic hair that looks shiny or too thick.

## What Breaks The Product
The product fails when it cannot stay on, cannot blend, or cannot survive normal use.

The survey is full of the same failure modes:
- band too big or not adjustable
- missing or weak clips
- broken strings or clasps
- bulky top piece that sticks out
- tangling, dulling, or dry ends
- curls not holding or heat damaging the hair
- slow shipping and bad support making a small issue feel like a scam

## What They Actually Want
They want something easy to install, easy to remove, and believable in real life, the same voice captured in the beauty brand customer voice.

The strongest positive language is:
- feels natural
- blends well
- gives me confidence
- makes me feel beautiful
- gives me fuller hair
- gets compliments
- saves time
- works for events and everyday wear

## What To Build Next
The product and the offer both need to reduce uncertainty.

Priority fixes:
- better color matching with samples or virtual try-on
- adjustable bands and smaller sizes
- more clips and stronger attachments
- clearer instructions, care notes, and install videos
- honest guidance on which textures and heat levels the hair can handle
- faster shipping and faster customer support, the same fix post purchase trust argues for
