# The Hero Product Video Structure

The hero product videos should follow a clear, repeatable structure that hooks attention fast, builds connection through personal storytelling, and drives action with a direct call to action.

## Hook First

Start strong. The first two seconds must grab attention. Use a surprising statement, a bold question, or a relatable pain point. Viral TikTok examples show that the best hooks are immediate and emotionally charged. The opener should make viewers stop scrolling.

## Personal Storytelling

After the hook, share a real moment from your life that ties into the core message. This builds trust and relatability. It doesn't need to be dramatic. Just authentic. People connect with vulnerability, especially when it leads to insight or transformation.

## Clear Call to Action

End with a specific next step: reply to this video, check out the link in bio, join the free guide, or comment your experience. Make it easy and urgent. The call to action should feel natural, not forced, and directly tied to the story you just told.

## Template from Viral Content

Use high-performing TikTok videos as structural blueprints. Study how top creators sequence their content: hook → story → CTA. Reverse-engineer what works, then adapt it to your own voice and brand.
