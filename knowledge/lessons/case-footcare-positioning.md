# Heel Pain Positioning

## The Better Anchor
Heel pain is a better anchor than a broad orthopedic category when the market needs something it can picture fast.

A vague label can be technically correct and still weak in copy. If the buyer knows exactly what their pain feels like when they wake up or try to walk, start there.

## Why It Worked
A few truths kept showing up.

- Neuropathy may be real, but it is harder to visualize and harder to sell
- Plantar fasciitis is common, but many buyers only know they have morning pain or walking pain
- The story is usually not the diagnosis. The story is the pain getting in the way of life
- A visible comfort demo helps the ad do the job faster

If the market can see the pain in its head, the ad has a better shot.

## How To Frame The Market
Match the message to awareness.

- Problem aware: my first steps hurt
- Solution aware: I need better support or better shoes
- Product aware: which orthopedic shoe is best for me

Do not skip the awareness step, the same ladder [[lead-types-and-awareness]] maps out. Different levels need different hooks.

## Creative Rules
A few practical rules came through clearly.

- Start with video before static if the account needs proof
- Use a visible comfort feature if the product has one
- Keep the headline tight before testing visual variations
- Make the story about getting life back, not just buying a shoe

The ad should feel like a person finding relief, not a brand making a claim, backed by the same rigor as [[specificity-and-exactness]].
