# The Content Creator System

A structured, repeatable approach to managing content creators that combines performance tracking with ongoing training to ensure consistent, high-quality output.

## Core Mechanics

The system centers on two pillars: accountability and development. Every creator is tracked from initial outreach through final delivery, with clear follow-up points at each stage. This includes checking in after the first draft is submitted and again once editing begins, ensuring nothing falls through the cracks.

## Performance & Follow-Up

Creators aren't just handed a task and left to their own devices. The system builds in structured touchpoints that keep momentum going. If a video isn't submitted on time, or if edits are delayed, the process triggers follow-ups that maintain pressure without burning out the creator.

## Training & Consistency

Over time, creators are trained to meet specific standards. This isn't just about one-off results; it's about building habits and expectations so that consistent output becomes the norm. The system evolves with each creator, refining feedback loops and onboarding steps based on what works.

## Integration With Broader Workflows

This system connects directly with other operations like content scheduling, editing workflows, and performance tracking. It ensures that every piece of content fits into a larger rhythm, from idea to publish, without friction.
