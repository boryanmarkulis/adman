# Green Means Scale, Red Means No

## The Rule
When a campaign or business state is clearly profitable, push it harder. When it turns red, stop pretending the old play still works and move into damage control.

## What Red Means
A red spell can come from a worse offer, rising CPA, stock issues, seasonality, or the platform changing under you. The key is not the explanation. The key is the margin.

If the control campaign is above break even, it is not a scaling problem anymore. It is a survival problem, the same read [[control-campaign-one-variable-testing]] is meant to catch.

## What To Do In Green
- Scale the control while it is still profitable
- Launch adjacent tests around the winning angle
- Capture as much upside as you can while the market is paying you
- Keep measuring so you know when the environment shifts

## What To Do In Red
- Cut waste fast
- Tighten spend or pause paid media if needed
- Protect cash and buy time
- Fix the actual problem instead of spraying money at it
- Use the red period to rebuild the system, not to fantasize about the old one coming back

## The Stop Loss Mindset
A hard stop loss is not quitting. It is a guardrail.

If the business falls through the guardrail, you decide with a clear head whether to revive it or walk away, the same discipline behind [[media-buying-is-not-the-lever]]. That choice is easier when you do not wait until panic has already drained the bank account.
