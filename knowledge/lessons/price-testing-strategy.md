# Price Testing Strategy

Testing price points is a low-friction way to validate customer perception and optimize conversion without changing the product or value proposition.

## How It Works

You duplicate an existing offer page and adjust only the price. This creates a controlled experiment where all variables except pricing remain identical. The goal is to measure which price drives better performance in terms of clicks, conversions, and overall ROI.

For example, the team tested $59.95 for the hero product product by duplicating the current offer page and updating just the price. This allowed them to compare performance directly against the original without introducing confounding factors.

## Key Setup Rules

- Always use a standardized price point across test variants.
- Include a clear **compare-at** price to anchor perception and reinforce value.
- Keep all other elements identical: copy, visuals, layout, CTA, and funnel flow.
- Use preview links or dedicated test pages to avoid mixing data.

## Why It Matters

Price isn't just about revenue. It influences how customers perceive quality, urgency, and fairness. A small shift in price can reveal hidden thresholds in customer behavior. Testing helps uncover the optimal balance between perceived value and conversion lift.

This approach fits into broader **ad-creative-testing** and **offer-architecture** workflows. It's not a standalone tactic but part of a larger system for refining pricing with data, not guesswork.
