# Product positioning is about clarity, not cleverness

A strong product position cuts through noise by stating exactly what the product does, who it's for, and why it matters in a way that feels obvious. It's not about marketing flair. It's about precision.

## The Sumbrella™ as a case study

The Sumbrella™ is positioned as a universal sunshade that covers the entire windshield and takes only seconds to install. It packs down to the size of an umbrella, making it easy to store and transport.

This positioning works because it answers three core questions immediately:
- What problem does it solve? Keeps your car cool in the sun.
- How is it different? Full coverage + instant setup + compact storage.
- Why should you care? No more hot seats, no fumbling with clips or straps.

## Clarity over complexity

You don't need a long story to make a product stick. The best positions are simple, specific, and rooted in real user benefits. If your product doesn't have a clear "what it does" statement, you're not ready to scale.
