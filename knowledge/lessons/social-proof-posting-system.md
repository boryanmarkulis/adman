# Social Proof Posting System

A consistent, visually clean social proof system boosts credibility and keeps your content looking professional across platforms.

## Template Rules for Visual Consistency

Always use a 1080px-tall template for square 1080x1080 posts. A taller 1440px template crops off the stat block, which ruins composition and readability. Adjusting to 1080px ensures all key data stays visible and aligned.

## Logo Discipline

Remove the brand logo from all future social-proof posts. Keeping it in creates visual clutter and makes reuse of the same project feel repetitive. A clean, logo-free design improves consistency and reinforces authenticity across your feed.

## Why It Matters

Clean templates with consistent spacing and no branding help your social proof feel trustworthy and scalable. When every post looks like it belongs to the same system, people notice the quality, not the distractions. This is part of building a high-leverage content rhythm that supports social media automation and the beauty brand ugc creative system.
