# Ad Spy Product Research

A way to see where buyers are already paying attention and where money is already moving, before you risk your own.

## What It Is

Ad spy isn't about copying. It's market reconnaissance. If someone is still spending money on an ad today, they likely believe it's working. That live signal is worth more than any brainstorm or gut feeling.

## Tools

PPAds and DropSpy are the two main tools the operator uses, both accessible through a Discord called Spy Essentials for around $30 a month. Afterlib is another powerful option that lets you track store revenue, pages, and ads side by side. You can search "advertorial" directly to uncover proven funnel structures.

## Search Setup

- **Platform**: Shopify  
- **Region**: US  
- **Language**: English  
- **Live status**: Running in the last 24 hours  
- **Time window**: Start with 3 months, then narrow down  
- **Sort by**: Impressions and shares  

The first filter is simple: is someone still spending money on this product right now? If yes, there's likely room. If no, keep moving. Sorting by shares reveals what people actually want to send to friends, not just what they glanced at once. High share counts signal organic desire layered on top of paid reach.

## What To Look For

Focus on products that:

- Solve a real, specific pain  
- Have margin left after ad spend  
- Are easy to understand in one visual  
- Target older or moneyed buyers  
- Are hard to find in regular stores  
- Offer a unique spin on an already proven product  

A live ad beats a screenshot. Shares matter because they show real desire.

## How To Judge It

Don't confuse saturation with dead ends. A crowded niche means you need a better angle, not a reason to quit. Ask: can you refine the offer? Target a more specific buyer? Simplify the message? A small twist on a proven product usually beats a fantasy invention.

If the niche is big but the product is thin, move on. Same stop-loss logic as store skeletons and the live-signal test in [[product-market-fit-research]].

Once a product passes the spy screen, run it through [[product-test-selection-heuristics]] to decide if it's worth testing, then into [[control-killer-cohort]] to pit it against your current winner.

## Benchmarks From Live Tests

These numbers come from a real ecom campaign in March 2023 and help calibrate expectations:

- **Cost per unique link click**: $0.66 to $0.79  
- **Unique CTR**: 1.75% to 1.82%  
- **CPM**: $11.95 to $13.95  
- **Website conversion rate**: 3.4% to 4.07%  

For a $44.95 product:
- **Cost of goods (individual)**: $9.20 → **Margin**: $35.75 → **Break-even ROAS**: 1.26  
- **Cost of goods (BOGO)**: $15.50 → **Margin**: $29.45 → **Break-even ROAS**: 1.52  

Know your numbers before judging a CPM as too high or low. Context is everything.

## Ad Creative Project

Once you find a product, the creative work begins. Expect to re-edit ads monthly and release new sets regularly. A typical project involves $5,000 upfront, plus $2,500 per month for ongoing ad re-editing and new creative batches. This keeps the messaging fresh and maintains momentum.
