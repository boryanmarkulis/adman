# AI Before/After Asset Hacking

AI image generation can multiply one good before/after into many ad-ready variants. The goal is not random art. The goal is fast, believable proof assets.

## Workflow
- Start with a strong base image and upload it as the reference.
- Ask AI to produce a detailed prompt for the exact transformation you want.
- Keep the before and after as close as possible on unchanged variables: subject identity, pose, lighting, clothing, and background.
- Change one variable at a time when iterating so you can see what improved or broke the result.
- Use the best output as a prompt template, then generate adjacent variants for testing.

## Why It Matters
- Cuts the time needed to produce social proof and ad creative.
- Lets one source asset become dozens or hundreds of testable variations, the same volume game [[case-beauty-content-engine]] plays
- Works especially well for skincare, hair, beauty, and other visual transformation offers, feeding the same test cycle as [[ad-copy-winning-patterns]].
