# The Beauty Brand Content System

## Content Pillars
The content system should rotate through a small set of repeatable pillars:
- Before and after
- Client testimonials
- UGC videos
- Hair care tips
- Product promotion
- Hair inspiration
- Color matching
- Memes and relatable hair jokes
- Hair and product showcases
- Motivational quotes

The point is not variety for its own sake. The point is to keep proving the same promise from different angles.

## Founder Role
The founder should own the angles, the offer, and the standards.

What stays on the founder:
- Media buying and ad ideation
- Choosing what the brand is really selling
- Deciding what content is worth testing
- Reading the numbers and killing weak ideas fast
- Coaching the team on what good looks like

What gets delegated:
- Customer support
- Repetitive editing and posting
- Outreach and creator tracking
- Routine reporting
- Ops cleanup

If the founder tries to do all of it, the content system turns into a bottleneck instead of an engine, the same trap [[case-beauty-content-engine]] warns against.

## Visual System
The brand guidelines are part of the system, not decoration.

Use:
- Fjalla One and Poppins
- #E61A95, #FAD1EA, #FFD051, #FFFFFF

Do not use:
- Animated content
- Politicians or celebrities
- Faces of other people that are not your own creators

The brand should feel clean, personal, and direct. No clutter. No fake polish.

## Caption System
Captions should be short and do one job: move the viewer toward action.

A good beauty brand caption usually does this:
- Names the problem or desire
- Shows the result
- Makes the offer feel easy and safe
- Ends with a direct CTA

Good angle language includes:
- Thicker and fuller hair in 60 seconds
- Salon-worthy hair at home
- Easy enough to throw on with zero experience
- Ethical extensions
- Zero damage extensions
- Money back if it does not work out

## Operating Rhythm
The content team needs a simple daily loop, not random bursts.

Useful rhythms from the source material:
- One new UGC per day
- 1 to 3 free orders per day
- Regular updates to the outreach tracker
- Daily end of day reporting to the content manager
- Clear status on which creators are in outreach, which packages are delivered, and which assets are still missing

The point is to keep the machine moving without guessing, the same tracking rigor in [[creator-outreach-system]].

## Proof Stack
The strongest content does not just describe the product. It stacks proof.

Use proof in this order:
- Social proof, reviews, and testimonials
- Creator demo videos
- Before-and-after visuals
- Simple installation and removal guidance
- Offer framing that lowers risk

The goal is to make the product feel like an obvious decision, not a leap of faith.
