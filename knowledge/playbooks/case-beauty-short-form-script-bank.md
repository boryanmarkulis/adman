# The Beauty Brand Short-Form Script Bank

## Core Angle Families
The strongest recurring angles in the beauty brand source material are:
- the truth behind the human hair trade
- why salon extensions suck
- why the viral hero product is putting salons out of business
- how the hero product stays in place
- how the product is made
- founder story
- ethical alternative and relief

These are not random topics. They are the repeatable ideas that already proved they can pull attention and move people toward the offer.

## Script Rules
The best scripts follow a tight format:
- less than 60 seconds
- one paragraph
- no more than 10 sentences
- below an 8th grade reading level
- conversational language
- no emojis
- a first line that hits hard immediately
- specific details, not vague claims
- a CTA that asks people to comment, share, tag a friend, or check out the shop

If the hook is weak, the rest of the script usually dies with it, the same taxonomy [[short-form-hook-taxonomy]] maps.

## Prompt Pattern
The source keeps reusing three useful voices.

human hair trade voice:
- journalist or exposé tone
- concrete sourcing details
- shock plus moral contrast
- end with awareness and sharing

Salon extensions voice:
- direct comparison between salon pain and the hero product ease
- price anchor, damage, time, discomfort
- end with shop CTA

Founder story voice:
- first person
- salon insider perspective
- emotional credibility
- clear reason the brand exists

## Production Notes
Do not treat the script bank like a brainstorm dump. Treat it like a reusable library.

When one angle works, make more versions by changing only the hook, the proof detail, or the CTA.

The most useful clips are the ones that can be remade fast from the same core story.
