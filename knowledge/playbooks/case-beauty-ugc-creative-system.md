# The Beauty Brand UGC Creative System

The goal is simple: keep a minimum of 5 new ads ready to test every day.

## System Goal

The working mix is:
- 3 new ads
- 2 iterations
- roughly 60 percent new creative and 40 percent iterations as volume grows

The system is built to keep the testing queue full, not to make one perfect asset. Delivery lag kills momentum, so the whole workflow is designed to stay ahead of the 20-day delay problem and feed the same queue the beauty brand creative testing loop runs on.

## Workflow

The loop has five parts.

1. Find and keep good creators.
2. Write briefs from research.
3. Receive deliverables in a usable format.
4. Turn deliverables into finished ads.
5. Send the ads into testing and read the results.

## Roles And KPIs

The work breaks into three jobs.

Creator management:
- keep 3 to 5 content creators producing consistently
- get a package every day or about 10 packages per month
- keep outreach going so the pipeline never runs dry
- ask for edits when needed and keep the process moving

Creative strategy:
- produce one new UGC brief every 3 days
- build briefs from surveys, reviews, forums, and customer language
- include 5 to 10 concepts per brief
- turn insights into ad angles the editor can actually use

Video editing:
- finish 5 ads per day per editor if the pipeline supports it
- match clips to the storyboard
- produce iterations from winning concepts
- submit the finished ads on time

## Brief Rules

A brief is only useful if it is specific enough to ship.

Good briefs should:
- use customer language
- state the hook and the job of the ad
- show what needs to be demonstrated
- include notes on install, back view, and final result if relevant
- label deliverables clearly
- keep mistakes visible so they can be corrected or ignored fast

Do not let creators lead the strategy. The team brings the angle, the proof, and the standards. Same discipline [[ugc-creator-outreach]] is built on.

## Creator Filming Instructions

Creators should hold up the hero product package, smile, and pan slowly toward it while keeping both themselves and the product in frame. The goal is natural and engaging, not staged.

At minimum 30 seconds of footage wearing the hero product is needed, showing how it blends with natural hair. The blend shot is the one that does the most work visually, so it needs time to breathe.

Creators are advised to include gifts or additional products in their footage to enhance the visual appeal and reinforce the value proposition.

## Script And Copy Standards

Copy should be as short as possible to make the sale. If a message can be delivered in one sentence, use one sentence. Start with the main idea, write multiple hook options, then pick the strongest ones.

The test for any line of copy: read it aloud. If it doesn't sound like a natural conversation, rewrite it. UGC copy that sounds written kills the format.

## Editing Standards

Match voiceover to clips tightly. Dead space kills retention, so cut it. The clips should serve the voiceover, not float around it.

Captions must match the natural tone of TikTok content. Avoid anything that looks like it was made in presentation software. Keep it casual and familiar.

Music matters more than it seems. Slow or low-energy tracks drag the ad even when visuals are strong. Choose upbeat, engaging tracks that match the pace of the feed and keep viewers from scrolling.
