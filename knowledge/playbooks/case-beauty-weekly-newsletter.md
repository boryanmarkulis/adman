# The Beauty Brand Weekly Newsletter

## What It Is
A weekly owned-media email that feels useful first and promotional second. The job is to give the reader one useful hair tip, one good article, three video links, one product highlight, and one reason to click.

## Build Order
Start from the last newsletter and clone it.

Use the title format:
`[Your initials] | Date | Newsletter | 30D Engaged`

Then fill the sections in this order:
1. Hair tip
2. Three top videos
3. Best article
4. Hot product
5. Subject line
6. Final QA and send time

## Newsletter Blocks
The newsletter should stay simple and repeatable.

Hair tip:
- Find one short tip from a hair-loss, extensions, or hair-pieces article
- Rewrite it into a clean standalone sentence
- Keep it to one or two sentences

Top videos:
- Pull the top 3 videos from Metricool for the last 7 days
- Use the viral hook headline or caption
- Link the headline
- Update platform and view count

Best article:
- Find one strong article on hair extensions or related hair advice
- Use the article title image or a screenshot
- Link the image to the article

Hot product:
- Pick the week's best product from social or Shopify sales
- Exclude the hero product when looking at Shopify
- Replace the title, image, and product copy so the section feels current, the same pillar rotation the beauty brand content system runs on

## Subject Line And QA
The hair tip should seed the subject line, using the same formulas as [[email-subject-line-formulas]].

Use the prompt:
`Write me 10 email subject lines that will get people to open the email and read this hair tip inside: [paste hair tip]`

Pick the catchiest line, then check:
- all links work
- all images point to the right place
- the mobile preview still reads clean
- the secondary subject line number increments correctly
- the send time is next Wednesday at 9am in the recipient's local timezone
