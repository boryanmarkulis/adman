# Creator Outreach for Hair Brands

## Who To Target
Prioritize creators who are deeply embedded in the haircare space and consistently produce engaging content. Look for those whose audience follows them specifically for hair-related topics, not just general lifestyle or beauty. The ideal candidate generates consistent traction with their content, often going viral on short-form platforms.

Creators who specialize in short-form video content and have a strong track record of high-performing reels are especially valuable. They understand the rhythm, hooks, and pacing needed to capture attention quickly. Their ability to deliver polished, platform-native content reduces friction in the collaboration process.

Follower count should not be a rigid gate. Instead, use it as one signal among many. Focus on engagement quality, content consistency, and audience alignment with your brand mission.

## Outreach Sequence
Start with a genuine, human touch. Lead with a specific compliment that shows you've actually watched their work. Explain why they're a fit based on their content style and audience, not just numbers.

Offer a gifted hero product with a clear ask: a brief review video and before-and-after photos. Keep the next step simple and low-pressure. Making a public post is optional unless required by the campaign.

If they respond, move them into the reply flow immediately. Collect shipping details and create the order without delay. If they request payment, keep the default as gifted unless the budget shifts.

## Follow-Up Cadence
Stay active but never pushy. One effective rhythm is:
- Initial message
- First follow-up after 24 to 48 hours
- Second follow-up after another 24 to 48 hours
- Shipping details follow-up if they replied but didn't complete the form

The goal is to keep the thread alive without feeling spammy. If someone isn't interested, close the loop and move on. Don't force it.

## Fulfillment Workflow
Once a creator agrees, trigger the mechanical flow:
- Create the order in Shopify
- Apply 100% discount for gifted collabs
- Mark as paid
- Set up a dedicated Google Drive folder
- Send the content brief and upload link
- After delivery, request the review video and before-and-after assets
- Once received, update the tracker and notify the content manager

This system works because it removes decision fatigue. Every step is repeatable and trackable.

## Tracking And Reporting
The tracking sheet is sacred. Update it daily with:
- First outreach sent
- Responded
- Interested
- Draft order created
- Product received
- Content received
- Google Drive link shared

End-of-day reporting must include:
- Number of outreach sends
- Number of draft orders created
- Whether the tracker is fully updated
- Number of products delivered
- Names of creators who submitted content

If it's not in the sheet, it didn't happen. This discipline mirrors [[creator-outreach-system]] and ensures accountability.

## Guardrails
Avoid relying on gut feelings or vague criteria. Use clear, repeatable rules instead.

Key guardrails:
- Favor creators who can make a simple, authentic review video without heavy direction
- Exclude salons or hair businesses unless the campaign targets them specifically
- Keep the offer and next step crystal clear
- Ask for content quickly but respectfully
- Use consistent templates so the team can scale efficiently, just like in [[ugc-creator-outreach]]
