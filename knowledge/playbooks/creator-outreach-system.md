# Creator Outreach System

A lightweight operating system for sourcing creators, sending tailored outreach, and keeping fulfillment and KPI tracking honest. The general version of [[creator-outreach-for-dtc-brands]].

## Sourcing

Prioritize Instagram Creator Marketplace plus platform search over broad cold lists.

Working filter: 5k+ Instagram followers or 30k+ TikTok followers, female 35+, US-based, actively posting within the last two weeks, one the hero product per influencer. No international creators for now.

For TikTok Shop specifically, use Creator Connect filters on hair care and styling rather than cold lists.

For paid UGC contracts, target macro and micro creators with a history of high-quality video work, including Caucasian women 35+ with thinning hair who are willing to do high-quality video. The content manager sources these, negotiates the smallest fee possible, and writes the scripts.

## Daily KPIs

- 20 to 50 creators sourced per day.
- 1 to 3 free product orders sent per day.
- 1 new UGC video delivered per day.
- The operating stretch target is 75 to 100 total outreach touches per day across platforms.

The KPI tracker uses conditional formatting so shortfalls show red immediately, without anyone having to calculate by hand. Track response rate (replies divided by first messages sent) and content collection rate (pieces received divided by first messages sent) as the two headline metrics. Keep these separate so weekly or monthly tests stay comparable.

## Outreach Sequence

Initial email structure: compliment a specific piece of their content, explain why they fit the brand, offer a free product in exchange for a review and before-and-after photos, keep posting optional, then a simple sign-off. Personalization matters more than volume here. Auto-filled usernames without any real reference to the creator's work kill response rates.

Follow-up cadence if no reply:

- 24 hours: first follow-up.
- 48 hours: second follow-up.
- 72 hours: third follow-up, then move on.

Standard handling for edge cases:

- Paid-only offer: decline warmly, note it is gifted-only for now.
- International creator: inform them shipping is US-only, keep them in the database for future campaigns.
- Slots full: send a graceful "we're at capacity" note and flag them for the next round.

## Tracking

Use a global outreach sheet and add creators at the bottom by date so the log stays chronological. Capture: handle, link, follower count, first outreach date, first response date, follow-up dates, interest status, order date, tracking number, and post date. Track the first response date separately from later follow-ups so first-touch response rate stays clean. Log metrics (views, likes) once content goes live. See also [[creator-outreach-operator-fit]] and the beauty brand ugc creative system.

## Fulfillment

Once a creator agrees, send the product and then:

- Share their dedicated Google Drive folder and ask them to upload video content plus before-and-after photos there.
- Provide their discount code and landing page link to include in their caption.
- Set a two-week posting window from the delivery date.

Follow-up cadence after delivery:

- 1 week out: check in if nothing has been uploaded.
- 3 days later: second reminder including the coupon code instruction.
- 3 days after that: final nudge before closing the loop.

When content is posted, send a thank-you note and an open invitation to collaborate again.

If shipment quantities need to change due to demand, notify the creator proactively before the order ships.

## Reviewer UGC Pipeline

Source 4 and 5 star product reviewers first, since positive reviewers convert at a higher rate. Invite them for a review-style video with before-and-after photos. Track status as: emailed, responded, content being made, submitted, to pay, paid. Keep one Drive folder for photos and one for videos, named by customer. If a reviewer is confused, send the detail sheet that covers deliverables, timeline, and examples, then follow up until the content arrives.

## Asset Syndication

Ask for raw footage and upload it to Drive so the asset can be reused. Repost finished videos across YouTube Shorts, TikTok, and Reels to stretch each creator piece across platforms. See [[ugc-creator-outreach]] for related practices.

## First-Touch Measurement

Track the first response date separately from later follow-ups so you can calculate a clean first-touch response rate. Add a dedicated response-date column and count replies against the original send date, not the follow-up date. Treat later replies as follow-up performance, not first-touch KPI. This keeps weekly or monthly message tests comparable.
