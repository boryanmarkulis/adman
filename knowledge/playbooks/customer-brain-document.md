# Customer Brain Document

## What It Is
A customer brain document is the living research file for a brand.

It is where you collect the phrases, pains, objections, proof points, and ad ideas that come out of real customer research. It is not a random notes dump. It is the place where raw signals get turned into usable marketing.

## What Goes In
Keep the document broken into a few clean buckets.

- Persona or avatar
- Main pain points
- Exact customer language
- Objections and skepticism
- What they already tried
- What they want instead
- Proof that matters to them
- Content angles that keep showing up
- Ad hooks worth testing
- Follow-up ideas and next questions

The key is to capture language as close to verbatim as possible. Real phrases beat polished summaries.

## How To Use It
Use the document to spot repetition.

If the same word, complaint, or desire shows up across calls, comments, and reviews, that is a signal, the same repetition test in [[review-mining-for-copy]]. Turn it into an angle, a headline, or a creative brief.

The best uses are:

- Pulling hooks for ads
- Writing landing page sections
- Naming objections before they show up in sales
- Finding new content to film or source
- Training AI to summarize patterns without losing the real language

AI can help, but only after the input is good. Garbage research in, garbage strategy out.

## Structure That Works
A simple structure is enough.

1. Persona summary
2. Top problems
3. Exact phrases from customers
4. Objections and fear language
5. Proof and trust signals
6. Winning angles and tests
7. New ideas to try next

Keep it updated daily or at least every time you get a meaningful new signal.

## What Not To Do
Do not make it a vague brand strategy doc.

Do not fill it with your own opinion.
Do not hide the raw language.
Do not start with weak external research when you already have direct buyer input.

The point is to build a brain that remembers what the market actually said, the same raw-signal discipline [[customer-research-before-copy]] runs on.
