# Embedded Customer Discovery
A pattern for getting real product insight by working inside the operator's business instead of guessing from the outside.

## The Pattern
Find a real operator with an actual business problem. Do not start with a generic market theory or a fake SaaS idea.

Offer immediate help in a form that matters to them, usually a retainer for implementation, marketing, systems, or operations. The point is to earn access to the real workflow.

Once you are inside, look at:
- Lead flow
- CRM hygiene
- Proposal history
- Follow-up gaps
- Objections
- Conversion rates
- What the owner actually complains about

## Why It Works
Inside access beats outside assumptions.

When you work with the operator directly, you stop guessing about what the market wants. You see the bottlenecks, the language customers use, and the places money is leaking.

That creates better product decisions and better sales decisions. It also makes the first version easier to sell because it is already anchored in a real business.

## How To Run It
Start small and specific:
- Get close to one operator
- Solve one obvious problem first
- Lock a baseline before trying to optimize
- Keep the relationship simple enough to stay in the business
- Build from the data you can actually see

For the studio, that meant getting into the solar installer through a monthly retainer for digital marketing and AI systems implementation, then using that access to shape the CRM the same way solar crm shallow adoption describes, proposal regeneration, and follow-up stack.

## Signals You Are Doing It Right
You know the pattern is working when:
- You get direct access to proposals and historical data
- The operator trusts you enough to tell you the ugly truth
- You can see real objections instead of abstract ones
- The work starts surfacing repeatable problems, the same reactivation opportunity proposal reactivation found inside the solar installer
- The product roadmap comes from the business, not your imagination
