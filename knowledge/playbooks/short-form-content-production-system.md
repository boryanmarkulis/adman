# Short-Form Content Production System

A repeatable way to turn source clips, UGC, news, or educational material into short-form videos that hold attention and drive action.

## What It Is
The system is simple: find a strong source, isolate the hook, rebuild the story with cleaner pacing, then add captions and a CTA that match the goal. The content is not the product. It is the delivery system for proof, curiosity, or conversion.

## Hook First
The first frame decides whether the video survives.

Use one clear hook:
- Problem recognition
- Curiosity gap
- Shock or contrast
- Transformation preview

The hook title should be able to stand on its own, pulled from the same buckets in [[short-form-hook-taxonomy]]. If it cannot, it is too vague.

## Edit for Retention
Keep the runtime tight.

- Remove filler words and dead air
- Change something every 2 seconds when possible
- Use B-roll to clarify, not to hide weak structure
- Keep the speaker visible when their face or delivery is the point
- Use sound effects and motion only when they support retention
- Cut the weak lines first if the video runs long

## Repeatable Formats
This workflow works across a few formats:
- Viral video curation: repost strong clips with clear attribution
- Educational remake: turn an authority clip into a B-roll voiceover
- Hook video: take the first few seconds of a strong clip and pivot into the solution
- News story remix: compress a story into a sharp narrative with a brand take
- UGC story: keep the human face, trim the drag, and support the story with tasteful B-roll

## Workflow
1. Pick the strongest source clip or story.
2. Write the hook in one sentence.
3. Trim the script to the minimum needed.
4. Generate or record the voiceover.
5. Edit in CapCut or the equivalent tool.
6. Add the hook title, captions, B-roll, music, and CTA.
7. Check the runtime and cut anything slow.
8. Export, upload, and log it in the tracker.

## Sourcing The First Three Seconds

- The strongest source clip is the one that creates instant curiosity or contrast in frame one. In the source notes, the repeated lesson is that the first three seconds matter more than the rest of the edit.
- Build a small library of `best hooks` folders by concept, then save the strongest clips there for reuse.
- Search TikTok and YouTube for odd, surprising, or high-contrast clips, download them, and file them by topic so future edits start from a stronger visual.
- Source: Short Form Post B-Roll Deep Dive 12/7/23

## Talking-Head and Story Remixes

- In creator-led remixes, keep the speaker visible when the face is the brand and use b-roll only to clarify or reset attention, not to decorate every line. Source: creator video and CTA SOP
- If the frame is already crowded with captions or face-framing, skip the hook title rather than forcing it in. Readability beats extra branding.
- For story remixes, start from one sharp narrative angle, cut filler aggressively, and replace slow sections with matched b-roll or a tighter line. Source: How To Create The Reddit Style Video Using CapCut 2/02/23, Source: Human Hair Trade News Story SOP 3/8/24
- Subtle CTA overlays work better than repeated hard sells when the edit is already doing the persuasion.

## Solar Batch Filming (Installer Vertical)

Evidence from the Media Scaling Circle course + the solar installer page performance data + July 2026 social scrape converges on a repeatable production model for solar installers:

- **3-evidence-stream brief**: build the brief from three sources simultaneously, brand's own page performance signals (which videos ran longest as paid ads), an industry social scrape (what formats pull engagement in the niche), and an expert course or playbook. All three before writing the first hook closes the gap between "something to film" and production-ready creative.
- **Time scope**: pitch 30-60 minutes total, pick 5 of 10 ideas, one shirt change between batches. That's one month of posts. Anything bigger (28 videos, multi-hour sessions) breaks the yes.
- **Top format in installer vertical**: real-install site walkthroughs running 9+ months as paid Meta ads. Carport walk-through is the template, replicate across 7 system scenarios (grid-tied, hybrid, off-grid, carport, rooftop commercial, villa, backup-only), the same repackaging instinct behind [[viral-content-sourcing-and-repackaging]].
