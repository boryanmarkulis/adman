# UGC and Creator Partnership Copy

Recruiting creators and partners to generate user-generated content (UGC) is a different task than selling products. The psychology is different. The messaging is different. The beauty brand's creator recruitment system shows what actually works at scale.

## The Core Psychology

Creators are already in your audience. They already know your product category. Your job is not to sell them the product. Your job is to show them a mutually beneficial partnership.

The pitch structure:
1. **Compliment their existing work** - Show you actually know their content
2. **Identify why they're a fit** - Explain why they matter for your brand
3. **State the offer clearly** - What you're giving them
4. **State the ask clearly** - What you need in return
5. **Make the next step easy** - Specific info to provide, clear CTA

## Email Subject Lines That Work

Based on the beauty brand's outreach at scale, subject lines that recruit creators:

**Direct and Enthusiastic:**
- "A Gift for Your Incredible Videos - Let's Collab!"
- "We LOVE your Content - Let's Collab!"
- "Collaboration Proposal Follow-up"
- "Partner With Us Again: We'd Love to Have You on Board!"

**Key Pattern:** The subject line should feel warm and personal, not corporate. It should signal that you've seen their specific work.

## The Initial Outreach Email Structure

### Version 1: Compliment + Value Prop + Ask

```
Hi [First Name]!

I stumbled on one of your incredible videos and was thoroughly impressed by your content. We would love to offer you a complimentary [product] of your preference from our collection.

We kindly ask only for a brief review and before-and-after photos. (Optional) You can also post your content on your social media accounts and earn!

If this is something that interests you, please lmk :)
```

**Why it works:**
- Opens with specific compliment (not generic praise)
- Immediately states the offer (free product)
- Makes the ask small and specific (review + photos)
- Mentions earning potential early (incentive to post)
- Casual sign-off (lmk :) ) builds warmth

### Version 2: More Personal, Identifies Fit

```
Hi [Name]!

I came across one of your videos and I'm really impressed with your content and creativity. I was wondering if you'd be interested in collaborating.

We would love to offer you one of our [product] of your choice from our collection.

We would only ask for a brief review and before-and-after photos. (Optional) You can also post your content on your social media accounts and earn!

If this is something that interests you, please lmk :)
```

**Difference:** Emphasizes their creativity and fit, asks about collaboration first. Feels like partnership, not transaction.

### Version 3: Full Email with Deliverables

When you want to move faster, include the information you need upfront:

```
Hi [Name],

I stumbled upon one of your incredible videos and was thoroughly impressed by your content. We would love to offer you a complimentary [product] of your preference from our collection.

We kindly ask only for a brief review and before-and-after photos, which we will provide you with a helpful guide for.

(Optional) You can also post your content on your social media account and earn!

To proceed, please send me your:
- First & Last Name
- Mailing Address
- Phone Number
- Which [product] color/style would match your [body part] best?
- Preferred length options

Once I get all the info, I can get the order processed today.
```

**Why it works:**
- Gets the information you need immediately
- Creates momentum ("get the order processed today")
- Specific deliverables remove ambiguity
- "We will provide a helpful guide" removes friction

## The Compliment Formula

The compliment must be specific and genuine. Creators can spot fake compliments instantly.

**Weak:** "Your content is amazing"
**Strong:** "I stumbled upon one of your incredible videos about [specific topic] and was thoroughly impressed"

**Weak:** "You're great"
**Strong:** "I really love all your content! I think it would be amazing if we could collaborate"

The compliment should:
1. Reference something specific they did
2. Use words that feel authentic (incredible, impressed, love, creativity)
3. Lead naturally into why you're reaching out

## Follow-Up Sequences

Creators are busy. Non-response is normal. A systematic follow-up sequence dramatically improves response rates.

### Timeline

**After 24 hours:** First follow-up
**After 48 hours (or 24 after first follow-up):** Second follow-up
**After 72 hours (if no response):** Third follow-up or abandon

### First Follow-Up (24 hours)

```
Subject: Collaboration Proposal Follow-up

Hi [Name],

Just checking back in. I emailed you a couple of days ago about a potential collaboration.

I stumbled upon one of your incredible videos and was thoroughly impressed by your content. We would love to offer you a complimentary [product] of your preference from our collection.

We kindly ask only for a brief review and before-and-after photos.

(Optional) You can also post your content on your social media accounts and earn!

Let me know if this is something you're interested in. I'm happy to discuss further.
```

**Key:** Keep it short. Reference the first email. Restate the offer but don't repeat all details.

### Second Follow-Up (48 hours from initial)

```
Subject: Collaboration Proposal Follow-up

Hi [Name],

I hope you're doing well. Just checking back in with the last email I sent.

We'd love to explore a potential collaboration with you.

Looking forward to hearing from you.
```

**Key:** This one is shorter. Simpler. You're signaling interest without being pushy.

### Silence for 5+ days: Shipping Details Follow-Up

If they engaged early but haven't provided info:

```
Hey [Name]!

It's been a while since our last conversation. I am eagerly awaiting your response since I wasn't able to get your shipping details previously to place your order. I am excited for you to try our [product]!

To proceed, please send me your:
- First & Last Name
- Mailing Address
- Phone Number
- Product choice and sizing

Once I get all the info above, I can get the order processed today.
```

**Key:** Assumes interest and moves toward action. Reduces friction by restating exactly what's needed.

## Following Up with Past Creators for Repeat Collaborations

Once a creator has done one collaboration, reaching out for additional work is completely different. They know you, they know the process, and there's less friction.

### Why Repeat Creators Are Valuable

**Faster onboarding:** They already know your process, standards, and payment terms.
**Proven quality:** You've seen their work. You know they deliver.
**Relationship foundation:** Trust is already built. They're more likely to say yes.
**Lower cost of acquisition:** No discovery or convince phase needed.

Repeat collaborations are your most efficient content channel.

### The Follow-Up Collaboration Email

```
Subject: Partner With Us Again: We'd Love to Have You on Board!

Hi [Name],

Greetings to you! I hope this message finds you well.

First and foremost, I want to extend my sincerest gratitude for the fantastic collaboration we had before. Your contributions were remarkable. Thank you so much!

We would like to invite you to join us in our mission of [mission statement - e.g., uplift women with thin hair to reclaim their confidence by achieving voluminous hair].

Details are as follows:
- We will provide you with a script for the content
- Submit video content within a week
- For content creation, we will send you $20 per video

Please let me know if you are interested. Thank you again for your outstanding work with us, and we look forward to hearing from you soon.

Best regards,
[Your Name]
```

**Why it works:**
- Opens with specific gratitude (not generic thanks)
- Names the mission (mission-driven motivation)
- Simplified offer ($20 per video is clearer than complex structures)
- Clear deliverables (removes ambiguity)
- Shorter and simpler than initial outreach

### Key Differences from Initial Outreach

**Initial outreach:** Lead with compliment, then offer free product + review request
**Repeat collaboration:** Lead with gratitude, then offer cash + specific deliverables

The first collaboration gets them to try your product and build the relationship. Repeat collaborations are straightforward business transactions.

### Post-Acceptance: Next Steps Email

```
Hi [Name],

Thank you for agreeing to another collaboration with us. We appreciate your willingness to work with us and look forward to working with you again.

Next steps:
- Please create your content using this video script [SCRIPT LINK]
- Video should not have background music or text captions
- Content is only dedicated to [brand] - no other brands mentioned/added
- Submit the video within 1 week

Please let me know if you have any questions.
```

**Key:** Specific deliverables. Removes any ambiguity about what you want.

### When Revisions Are Needed

```
Hi [Name],

I wanted to take a moment to express our appreciation for the incredible content you produced for our product. Your creativity and dedication to quality really shone through in the work you delivered.

While we are thrilled with the overall video, we have a small request for a revision. Specifically, we were hoping that you could [INSERT SPECIFIC CHANGES].

Please let us know if this is possible, and if there is any additional information or context you need from us to make these changes. Thank you again for your hard work and commitment to delivering top-notch content.

Best regards,
[Your Name]
```

**Why it works:**
- Opens with genuine gratitude for what they did
- Compliments the work specifically ("creativity and dedication")
- States revision request clearly and specifically
- Shows deference ("small request", "if this is possible")
- Offers help if needed

This maintains goodwill while getting what you need.

### When Video Is Approved

```
Hi [Name],

I just wanted to express our gratitude for the wonderful content you created for our product. We are in awe of the creativity and hard work you put into making the content!

Please provide me with your PayPal where we can send the compensation.
```

**Key:** Keep it simple. Lead with genuine appreciation. Moving to payment/next logistics.

## Objection Handling in Creator Emails

### International Creator (Won't Ship There)

```
I appreciate your reply. I noticed that your address is based in [Country]. Unfortunately, at this time, we are unable to send products internationally. We would love to keep you in mind for future collaborations if our marketing needs change or if we expand our operations to your region. Thank you so much for your time.
```

**Key:** Respectful decline that leaves door open. Keeps them in database for future.

### Creator Wants Payment

```
I really appreciate your reply. We fully respect your rates, but unfortunately, as a small business, we could only offer a gifted collaboration right now. We'd love to send you one of our [product] on our website for free. All we'd like to request is a short, simple review (we will provide you with a helpful guide).

(Optional) You can also post your content and earn 10% commission on sales for 30 days when customers use your code!

Please let me know if you'd like to proceed.
```

**Key:** Validate their rate. Reframe as value exchange (free product + potential earnings). Make it easy to say yes.

### High Demand, Slot Filled

```
So sorry, we have received an overwhelming amount of responses regarding this opportunity. We actually have filled all of our slots for our collaboration, but we would love to work with you in the future. Your information is stored in our database, and we will let you know of any future campaigns!

Thank you so much!
```

**Key:** Scarcity actually increases future interest. Keeps door open.

## Incentive Framing: The Earn Structure

The beauty brand structures incentives to encourage both review UGC and posting.

### The Pitch

```
We would love to offer you a complimentary [product]. We kindly ask only for a brief review and before-and-after photos.

(Optional) You can also post your content on your social media accounts and earn! You will receive 10% of your total sales for 30 days when a customer makes a purchase using your coupon code.
```

**Why this works:**
1. **Lead benefit:** Review + photos (most important, non-negotiable)
2. **Bonus benefit:** Earn if you post (incentivizes amplification)
3. **Specific commission rate:** 10% is concrete, believable
4. **Specific time window:** 30 days removes open-endedness
5. **Specific mechanism:** Coupon code (tangible, trackable)

### Coupon Code Messaging

Once they receive product and are ready to post:

```
Here's your coupon code: [CODE] (Buy 1 Get 1 + Free Shipping)

Include this in your caption so your followers can take advantage of the deal. You'll receive 10% commission on any sales using your code for 30 days.

Who wouldn't adore this deal!
```

**Key:** The offer itself becomes the selling point. The creator is giving their followers a deal while earning commission. It's a win-win.

## Post-Delivery Follow-Up

### After Confirmed Delivery

```
We see that your package was delivered to you. A gentle reminder:

- Please check this Simple Review Video brief: [GUIDE_LINK]
- Kindly upload the unedited video and before-and-after photos to your assigned Google Drive folder
- Please inform me once you've finished uploading

You can also post your content to your social media accounts and earn! Your coupon code: [CODE] (Buy 1 Get 1 + Free Shipping). Include this in your caption.

Lmk if you have any questions! We're excited to see what you create!
```

**Key:** Specific next steps. The brief removes ambiguity. Reiterates earning potential.

### When Creator Posts

```
I wanted to take a moment to express my sincere gratitude for your recent collaboration. The content was awesome and you look amazing!

Your professionalism, creativity, and enthusiasm were commendable and we couldn't be more thankful. We appreciate everything you've done to make this collaboration happen.

Thank you!
```

**Key:** Specific praise (professionalism, creativity, enthusiasm). Gratitude without gushing. Opens door for future collaboration.

## Voice and Tone Principles

Creator outreach is different from product sales. The tone should feel like partnership, not transaction.

### What Works

- **Warm and enthusiastic:** "We LOVE your content!"
- **Personal:** Use their first name, reference specific content
- **Specific:** Name what impressed you, what you're asking for
- **Casual:** "lmk :)" feels more genuine than "Please advise"
- **Grateful:** "We really appreciate..." "Thank you so much!"
- **Emoji strategic:** Heart emoji at end of friendly emails, exclamation points
- **Non-corporate:** No "Dear Sir or Madam" energy

### What Doesn't Work

- Generic praise ("Your content is great")
- Corporate tone ("We would be delighted to engage your services")
- Salesy language ("special offer" feels cheap)
- Overly casual (no text-speak like "ur" or "thru")
- Unclear asks (make it specific and small)

## The Selection Criteria

Before you even reach out, the beauty brand uses these filters:

- **Follower count:** 5k+ for Instagram, 30k+ for TikTok (has reach)
- **Age:** 35+ (matches product demographic)
- **Recent activity:** Posted within last 2 weeks (actively engaged)
- **Geography:** US-based only (shipping, legal)
- **Content fit:** Content about hair health, beauty, aging (relevant audience)

**Why this matters for copywriting:** Your message changes based on their content. You reference what they actually posted. Specificity increases response rate.

## Testing Variables

You can test different approaches in creator outreach:

- **Subject line variations:** Different hooks
- **Compliment specificity:** Generic vs. detailed reference
- **Incentive mention placement:** Lead with it vs. bury it
- **Ask clarity:** Small ask vs. comprehensive ask
- **Tone:** Warm vs. professional

Track which approaches get highest response rate and follow-up conversion. The pattern will emerge quickly with 20-30 outreaches.

## Common Mistakes

**Generic praise:** "Your content is amazing" gets ignored. "I stumbled on your video about [specific topic] and was impressed by how you explained [specific thing]" gets responses.

**Unclear ask:** Don't say "interested in collaborating." Say "review + photos. optional: post for commission."

**Burying the incentive:** Put the compensation opportunity in the initial email, not as a surprise later.

**Too many follow-ups:** After 3 (initial + 24hr + 48hr), move on. More emails look spammy.

**Not delivering on the brief:** If you promise a guide, provide it. If you promise quick processing, process quickly.

**Forgetting to make it about them:** The best creator emails focus on THEIR benefit, not your need for content.

## Scale

The beauty brand's daily KPI is 1 new UGC per day, which means sending 20-50 outreaches daily (rough 2-5% response conversion).

At scale, the same contract discipline ugc creator agreement formalizes once a creator becomes a repeat partner:
- The system matters more than any single email
- Consistency beats perfection
- Small improvements compound (better subject line = +10% response = +3 monthly collaborations)
- You find patterns in objections that inform future outreach
