# UGC Creator Outreach

## What It Is
A repeatable system for sourcing, qualifying, briefing, shipping, and following up until usable content is in hand. The goal isn't reach or follower count. It's real-looking, relatable content that feels like a genuine customer experience.

## Who To Recruit
Fit trumps polish. You want creators who look and sound like your buyer, not just those with high production value.

The ideal UGC creator:
- Resembles the target customer (age, style, life stage)
- Feels authentic on camera, even if their editing isn't pro-level
- Is active and consistent in posting
- Can make the product feel normal and believable

For hair brands like the beauty brand, this means focusing on women who are close to the 50+ menopause hair-loss buyer. A slightly imperfect but relatable creator is better than a flawless one who doesn't resonate.

There are two key archetypes:
- The more polished, aspirational woman
- The real-life mom or micro-influencer who feels like someone you'd actually talk to

Prioritize the latter when building trust and authenticity in your content. Even if their content isn't high-end, consistent posting and genuine engagement matter more than perfection.

## Outreach Flow
Keep it short, specific, and warm.

Start with a real compliment on their recent work. Then explain why they're a fit for your brand. Offer the collab as gifted or paid based on your model. Ask clearly for exactly what you need: video, before-and-after photos, unedited clips.

If no reply:
- First follow-up after 24 hours
- Second follow-up after 48 hours

If they ask for payment and it's a gifted collab, say so directly and move on. No awkwardness.

## Fulfillment And Tracking
Once they agree, keep the process tight.

Use Google Sheets to track every creator. Build the order, confirm shipping details, send the brief, create a dedicated Drive folder, and assign deadlines. Send the coupon or affiliate link after the product ships.

The assistant should be a follow-up machine: tracking delivery, nudging when needed, and ensuring content lands in your system on time.

## Content Rules
Be specific so the output is usable.

Ask for:
- The product being used, not just held up
- Install and removal shown if relevant
- Unedited video and before-and-after photos
- No music or text overlays unless requested
- Brand tag in post (if applicable)
- Coupon code in caption (if applicable)

A simple, authentic demo from the right person beats a flashy clip from someone who doesn't fit. This is the same discipline used in the beauty brand ugc creative system.

## Content Organization
Sort UGC by shade to build accurate before-and-after comparisons.

Create a new folder for each color variant: Ash Brown, Beach Blonde, etc. This makes it easy to test and show real results across different tones. It also supports better product testing and more targeted marketing.

This system is critical for brands where visual transformation matters, like hair or skincare.
