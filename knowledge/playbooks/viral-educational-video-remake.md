# Viral Educational Video Remake

A workflow for turning a viral educational short into a branded ad without reinventing the concept, a narrower case of [[viral-content-sourcing-and-repackaging]].

## Core Pattern
- Start with a video that already proved the topic, hook, and authority style.
- Rebuild it with a matching voiceover, branded captions, and b-roll that supports the narration.
- Keep the piece educational first, promotional second.

## Workflow
1. Find a viral clip in the exact niche and verify the hook, authority figure, and engagement.
2. Pull the transcript in Descript or a similar tool.
3. Rewrite only enough to fit the offer and brand.
4. Generate a new voiceover in ElevenLabs.
5. Assemble the edit in CapCut with b-roll changes every few seconds.
6. Match visuals to the spoken claim. Do not let random footage drift away from the narration.
7. Finish with branded captions, light motion, music, and a CTA.

## Editing Rules
- Prefer videos with a clear teaching structure, not just aesthetic talking-head clips.
- Use high-quality footage. Weak b-roll drags the whole piece down.
- Remove dead space and keep the pacing tight.
- If the source video already went viral, preserve its pacing and logic more than its exact wording, the same source-first rule in [[short-form-content-production-system]].
