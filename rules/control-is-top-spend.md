# The control is the ad with the most spend

## Rule

The control is the ad holding the most spend over the last 3 days, ending yesterday. Spend alone selects it. No minimum age, no minimum spend, no cost-per-result sort, no significance test.

Then go one level down. Inside a dynamic-creative ad, take the top-spend image, headline and primary text. Those are what the challenger holds.

## Why

Ranking by cost per result picks noise. On one real account the top three ads sat 7% apart on 5 to 12 leads each, so the "winner" flipped every morning. Three days running the loop iterated on a low-volume ad while most of the budget sat on a different one. Three rounds taught nothing.

The platform's delivery system sees far more signal than a 3-day export. Trust it. Do not try to outsmart it or argue where budget should have gone.

Stopping at the ad level also misleads. Real concentrations: 91 to 99% of an ad's spend on one image, 82% on one primary, 96% on a headline that was not the one visible on the post.

## Apply

- If the spend leader is not the results leader, keep it as the control and name the gap as the round's problem: it wins delivery, not conversion.
- A brand new ad set that took the budget today is the control today.
- If nothing spent, stop and say so.
- Cost per result, CTR and frequency still go in the round doc. They never choose the control.
