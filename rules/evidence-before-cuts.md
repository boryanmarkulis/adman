# Evidence before cuts

## Rule

Do not kill a creative, an angle, an audience, or a placement on a short or thin slice.

Before any cut, confirm: enough time, enough volume, and a verified definition of the outcome you are measuring.

## Why

A "zero conversions" segment turned out to be three clicks. Cutting it removed a real segment on noise. Short windows manufacture confident nonsense.

## Apply

- Look at six months before trusting thirty days.
- State the sample size out loud in the recommendation.
- Hook rate moves fast and can be judged early. Conversion rate cannot.
- Say "not enough data yet" when that is the honest answer. It is a real finding.
