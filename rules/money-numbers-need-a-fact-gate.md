# Every money number in an ad needs a dated fact-gate entry

## Rule

Any number in an ad, landing page or message about what something costs, saves, earns back, or how long it takes to pay off must come from `research/fact-gate.md`, with a verified date and the rules it assumes. If it is not there, it does not ship. Ship the idea without the figure, or ship nothing.

A customer quote, review, forum post or old call supplies **words**, never money numbers.

## Why

A real ad told readers what a "fair" payback period was and invited them to judge their own quote against it. It was the account's best creative. The range came from four forum comments. Three of the four predated a large incentive that had since ended, so their math assumed a discount no buyer could get any more. The number never passed a fact check, because the pipeline only checked whether a line was verbatim, not whether it was still true.

The problem was not that forums are unreliable. The economics changed under a number that looked fine, and nothing noticed.

## Apply

Before any money number ships, answer in the round doc:

1. When was this number produced? When the person said it, not when you read it.
2. What rules was it computed under? Prices, incentives, tax treatment, rates, financing terms, seasonal pricing.
3. Are all of those still true today? If any one changed, the number is dead. Get a new one.

Re-check any fact-gate entry older than 90 days before reuse. When mining a corpus, harvest price and savings lines for their phrasing only.
