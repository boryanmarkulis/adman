# Check a creative hunch before it changes the ad

## Rule

When the operator offers a feeling about an ad ("that angle feels niche", "people only look at the image"), treat it as a hypothesis. Check it, report what the evidence says, including where it disagrees, then act on the evidence.

Explicit decisions ("use this image", "publish tomorrow") are followed directly. This rule is for hunches.

## Why

Applied literally, a passing remark became a rule and reshaped a round the evidence did not support. The operator's own correction: do the research first, then act.

## Apply

Evidence, in this order:

1. Past experience: `knowledge/`, past round docs and their results.
2. Real customer words: `research/copy-corpus.md`, ad comments, sales call notes, reviews.
3. The campaign's own results: past ads that used the idea, and how they did.

Keep it short. Three lines of evidence, then the recommendation.
