# Track C seed bank template

Copy to `research/track-c-seed-bank.md` and make it about your buyer.

These are **looks**, not reasons. Use a seed to dress a new Track C angle, never as the reason for a round. A new look on the same reason is Track B.

Open it once the Track C angle is chosen. Take the top open row. Mark it BUILT with its round folder afterwards. Add any new branch the round suggested. A seed is not spent until its whole family is.

Try a comparison first (before and after, this versus that). Then this bank, then the Ads Library worldwide.

The test for a good seed: **something your buyer already looks at without suspicion**, that does not look like an ad.

```markdown
| Seed | Grown branches | Status |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Text message from a friend | group chat with three neighbours, community group post, voicemail transcript, forwarded screenshot | open |
| Phone note | calculator on the counter, browser tab bar, a to-do list | open |
| Sticky note | fridge magnet, wall calendar with a day circled, kitchen whiteboard, note taped to a bill | open |
| Bumper sticker | lawn sign, window decal, hand-lettered shop sign, sandwich board | open |
| Person holding a sign | person pointing at something, handwritten cardboard sign, protest-style placard | open |
| Newspaper in someone's hand | paper on a table next to a bill or receipt, clipped article on a fridge | open |
| Reporter with a microphone | weather-map graphic, scrolling news ticker, anchor-desk two-shot | open |
| Doorbell camera clip | someone walking up, walking away, the app's event list | open |
```

## Rules that keep it honest

- One direction holds many executions. Write the specific execution and why that one, for this buyer, now.
- An execution is used once. The seed stays open until its whole family of branches is built. Check `research/round-image-inventory.md` first.
- Never impersonate a real outlet, authority, platform or brand. A made-up masthead must be clearly fictitious.
