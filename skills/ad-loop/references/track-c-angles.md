# Track C angle log template

Copy to `research/track-c-angles.md`. One row per Track C round. Read it before picking a new angle.

```markdown
| Date | Round | Act now because | Belief | Level | Source | Result (spend / results / cost) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 2026-10-12 | 2026-10-12c-vs-split | your bill and your neighbour's are not the same | the bill looks wrong | Problem aware | news, outlet, date | fill in later |
```

## How to read it

- The same belief three days running on the same kind of image is the rut. Pick something else.
- If the last three rounds sat on one or two levels, pick a level not tried lately.
- When a round wins spend and results, mark it. Over a few weeks the level column tells you where the audience sits.
