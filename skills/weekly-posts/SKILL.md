# Weekly Posts

Create a coherent week of content without forcing weak slots.

## Process

1. Read `SUBJECT.md`, `VOICE.md`, recent files in `work/queue/`, and the latest `research/inspo/` report when present.
2. Propose the week before drafting. For each slot give the date, channel, format, objective, angle, and required asset.
3. Get approval for the plan.
4. Draft each approved slot. Use `brand-graphic` for static visuals and `video-ad` for paid or scripted vertical video.
5. Save each slot under `work/queue/<YYYY-MM-DD>/` with `copy.md` and any creative brief or asset.
6. Review the week for repeated hooks, unsupported claims, missing assets, voice drift, and conflicting calls to action.

This skill prepares a queue only. Scheduling and publishing are separate actions and require explicit authorization.
